## Why

A synthetic voice is indistinguishable from a human one (Edison/SSRS, May 2026:
61%). Synthesis will pronounce the correct and the incorrect price with equal
confidence — intonation confidence is not tied to content accuracy. An error
now sounds flawless. The catch moves from *before recording* to *after
synthesis*. A voice acquires a **release moment** — like a newspaper, a radio,
a live broadcast.

## The release principle

> Recording is a commodity. Responsibility is never.

The decision to publish **always remains with the client**. The studio is the
**last professional filter**: it verifies that the decision is made on checked
material. The studio is not the responsible party for content.

## The pre-air check (редактура голоса до эфира)

A human answers the question «can this be released?». The check covers:

1. **Figures** — tariffs, numbers, dates: verify against the client's text.
2. **Wording** — formulations, names, legal phrasing.
3. **Accents / pronunciation** — names, foreign words, industry terms.
4. **Usage rights** — rights volume, consent, boundaries (see
   ../standards/voice-provenance-passport.md).
5. **Final version** — the exact version released to air, hash-locked
   (see ../standards/verification-chain.md).

## The release procedure

| Step | Owner | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Check | studio | verify figures, wording, accents, rights, final version |
| 2 · Passport | studio | issue voice provenance passport (../standards/voice-provenance-passport.md) |
| 3 · Decision | client | decision to publish (always the client's) |
| 4 · Lock | studio | final version hash-locked; release through the check |
| 5 · Record | studio | verdict journal entry (../corpus/verdicts-journal.md) |

If a finding is material (wrong figure, wrong name, unlicensed use), the
release is **held**: the material returns to the client for correction, not
released "as is".

## The release boundary

- **Client** — decides, and answers for content (responsible party per passport).
- **Studio** — checks professionally, and answers for the check's quality.
- **Technology** — synthesis, dubbing, cloning services act under the same
  boundary: their output is not a release until checked and decided.
