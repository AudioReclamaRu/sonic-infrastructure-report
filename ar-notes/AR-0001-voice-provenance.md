# AR-0001 Voice Provenance

Definition
Voice Provenance is the verifiable record of a voice asset's origin: who
recorded it (or which model synthesized it), when, with whose consent, and
under what usage boundaries. It compensates for the disappearance of the
argument "this was recorded by a live narrator" in a market where a synthetic
voice is indistinguishable from a human one (61%, Edison, May 2026 — E-2026-014).

Scope
Where it applies: any published voice asset — IVR, advertising, training,
podcasts, dubbing. Where it does not apply: instrumental/nature sound without
a voice, declared as such.

Required fields
- Evidence ID: E-2026-014
- Canonical term: Voice Provenance
- Minimal metadata: source, date, confidence (p = 1,0 / lower)

Minimal compliance checklist
- Evidence ID present

Example usage
A bank labeling its IVR voice states: provenance tier, consent, and the party
answering for the release; a listener can verify authenticity within 3 working
days.
