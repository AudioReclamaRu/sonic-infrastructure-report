# AR-0002 Releasing Voice

Definition
Releasing Voice is the human act of approving a voice asset before publication,
while the final responsibility remains with the client. Release is a public
commitment, not a sound: the decision to publish binds the person — whether
the asset is synthetic or human.

Scope
Where it applies: the pre-air stage of any voice asset that becomes part of a
company's voice. Where it does not apply: personal, non-public recordings.

Required fields
- Canonical term: Releasing Voice
- Minimal metadata: what was checked (figures, wording, accents, rights,
  final version), who answers for the release
- Acceptance: the publish decision always remains with the client

Minimal compliance checklist
- The responsible party is named
- The pre-air check procedure was run (see standards/responsible-release.md)
- Provenance is attached to the released version

Example usage
A company's IVR recording is checked by a studio, then released through the
client's decision; the studio is the last professional filter, never the
responsible party.
