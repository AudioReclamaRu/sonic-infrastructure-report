# Today's Interface — 2026-07-08

**Type:** Daily Signal · **Audience:** Director · **Last updated:** 2026-09-11
**Status:** Signal

## What happened

OpenAI launched GPT-Live: voice became a system-control interface, replacing
Advanced Voice Mode for ~150M paying users (evidence/E-2026-011.md).

## What this changes

- The voice a customer already trusts for control is now the same interface your
  IVR competes against — a context shift, not a trend.
- A company's recorded voice is no longer competing with other recordings; it
  competes with the operating system's voice.
- Procurement conversations drift from "how it sounds" to "what it controls".

## What to check

- [ ] Does any internal policy cover voice-as-interface usage — not just content?
- [ ] Who in the company can approve a voice that "controls" software?
- [ ] Where does your voice asset sit — content stack or control stack?
