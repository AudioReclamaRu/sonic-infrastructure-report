# Signal 2026-07-29

What happened
Share of Ear (Edison Research at SSRS, 29.07.2026): AM/FM radio retains a
significant lead in time-spent-listening over ad-supported Spotify; perception
among advertisers diverges from actual listener behavior.

Why it matters
- Listening behavior and advertising perception are drifting apart — the
  "map" (market metrics) no longer matches the territory (actual time spent)
- For media planning: a reach-optimized campaign can miss where attention
  actually lives

What to check tomorrow
- Compare your channel plan with time-spent data, not with industry talk
