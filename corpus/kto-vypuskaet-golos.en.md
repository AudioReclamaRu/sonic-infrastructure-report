Your company already has a voice. It goes on air. Who checked it before release?

While we argued whether AI can sound human, the market answered. In a blinded
study most listeners could not tell a synthetic voice from a live one. So the
question is no longer how a voice sounds. It is what a human checks.

GPT-Live made voice an interface. AI dubbing became production. Recording stopped
being scarce.

---

Synthesis will deliver a correct and an incorrect rate with identical confidence.
To the model both numbers are equal — vocal confidence is not connected to
accuracy of content. Errors now sound flawless.

The error used to be caught before recording. Now it is caught after synthesis.
A voice acquires a moment of release — like a newspaper, radio or broadcast.
And the market already has a word for it.

Editing. Not literary — editorial. A person who checks and answers the question
"can this be released?"

That is why we stopped billing the service in minutes. We bill the check that
happens before a voice becomes a company's voice.

Verify the figures. Check the wording. Stress. Usage rights. The final version
of the recording.

The decision to publish remains yours. Our work is to make sure that decision
is made on verified material.

618 voices. 62 languages. One principle: before a voice becomes a company's
voice, someone checks it.

Synthesis can say any text with the same confidence. So before release a new job
appears: verify figures, wording, stress, usage rights, final version.

The decision to publish remains with the client. Our work is that the decision
is made on verified material.

This is where sound recording for business begins.
