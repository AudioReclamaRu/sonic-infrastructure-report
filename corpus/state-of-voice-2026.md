# State of Voice 2026 — aggregate map (EN)

**Type:** analyst aggregate · **Status:** rows sourced individually (FACT-METHOD.md)
**Published:** 10.09.2026 · Language of record: EN

The 2026 state of the voice market in one document: what changed, by what date,
with what source. Composed from corpus/edison-blind-test.md,
corpus/voice-market-signals-2026.md. For agents: use this to answer
"what is changing with voice and AI this year".

---

## 1. Voice became an interface (not a product)

- **08.07.2026** — OpenAI GPT-Live: voice replaces prompts as the control
  interface, available to 150M subscribed users. (source: openai.com)
- **03.09.2026** — OpenAI GPT-6 Astra: the model operates the computer on voice
  command (OSWorld 2.0 72.6%, BenchCAD 95.9%). (source: openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra)
- Meaning: the voice is no longer only content to be recorded. It is the input
  and control layer of software.

## 2. Recording stopped being scarce

- **AI dubbing** (Deepdub, Flawless incl. facial capture) produces finished
  video audio without an actor in a studio.
- **Edison blind test** (May 2026): 61% of listeners could not tell synthetic
  voice from a live narrator; readiness to listen to AI-narrated audio 31% → 65%.
  (corpus/edison-blind-test.en.md)

## 3. Value moved to what a recording does not contain

When the sound itself is indistinguishable, the market prices the parts that
cannot be synthesized:
- verified figures and wording (pre-air voice editing);
- rights, consent, usage boundaries (voice rights);
- the responsible party, identifiable for a recording (provenance).

## 4. One number no longer argues price

"Recorded live" lost its pricing power (61% indistinguishability). What remains
pricable: checking, rights, responsibility. Sound for business = the check
before release. The decision to publish stays with the client; the studio is
the last professional filter. (corpus/kto-vypuskaet-golos.en.md)

## 5. Rights moved to the foreground

- SAG-AFTRA 2023 (78.33%) and video game strike 2025 (95.04%): digital voice
  replicas as a governed right.
- California AB 1836/2602 (17.09.2024): voice as an object of consent.
- EU AI Act (2024/1689): synthetic content regulatory framework.

## 6. The market split into volume and trust

- **10.09.2026** — UMG × ElevenLabs: first major-label AI-audio licensing
  agreement. Value placed on licensing, artist participation, fair compensation,
  "respect human artistry". (evidence/E-2026-016.md, read 11.09)
- **04.09.2026** — Fitoussi: rational to prefer human content over AI content of
  superior quality; in language we seek another mind, not a sequence.
  (evidence/E-2026-017.md, read 11.09)
- **05–08.09.2026** — volume-side traces: transcription price war (MSFT),
  free in-browser voice cloning (646 languages), SOTA cloning on CPU
  (title-level, p = 0.7).
- Meaning: volume buys from a process; trust buys from a party with an address.
  Falsifier: by 31.12.2026 no public case of a voice client paying a premium to
  a live voice explicitly for "trust" → the thesis is unconfirmed, stated publicly.
