## Definition

> Any system that measures an experience, by optimizing the mechanism of the
> experience, risks replacing the experience with its own metrics.

The axiom is not new. The consequence was understood late: a metric that
serves a mechanism eventually begins to serve itself, and the orchestra
plays precisely, in time, note for note — but not *it*.

**Shorter:** the map (metrics) must serve the territory (experience), not the
other way round.

---

## The four stages

| Stage | What happens | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Experience | A person recognises himself; «a place was left for me». | «this moves / this doesn't» |
| 2 · Model | A hypothesis of what CAUSES the experience (Do → Re → Mi…). | «the mechanism is described» |
| 3 · Metric | The hypothesis becomes falsifiable (M-Do, M-Live, M-Pulse…). | «criteria fixed before data» |
| 4 · Turn | The model starts serving the metric. Experience is no longer checked — checkboxes are. | «we did everything right — but where is the recognition?» |

The turn happens on stage 4. The metric created as a SERVANT of experience
becomes its MASTER.

---

## The falsifier (how to detect the error)

Diagnostic question:

> Can a project pass all its metrics and NOT produce the experience?

If yes — the metrics measure the mechanism, not the experience; the project is
in the turn.

---

## The cure: a tuning fork, not more metrics

Add one metric that measures not the image but the *human transition* —
**M-PR** in the studio's method:

1. At what moment did you stop looking at the frame and start looking at yourself?
2. Was there the feeling that a place was left for you?
3. After which frame did you want to stay in silence?

M-PR is a **tuning fork**: it is not the symphony itself, and it does not
measure the mechanism. It calibrates everything else — prompts, labels, form —
until the experience actually occurs. If M-PR fails, all other checkboxes are
meaningless: the orchestra is out of tune, too early to play.

> Any system that measures an experience must have a metric that cannot be
> passed without experiencing the experience itself.
