# Declaration of Hutzpa

A new term enters the canon only if it makes the previous way of speaking about
the problem impossible.

Not elegant, not fashionable. Because the market adopted the old vocabulary —
"AI voice", "synthetic", "engagement", "unique voice" — to *not* speak about
what matters: who is responsible for what is released, what the listener can
verify, who has the right to be present. A word that coexists with that language
changes nothing. A term that removes the comfortable sentence from the industry
does.

## What the five operators made impossible

| Operator | The sentence it removed |
|---|---|
| Releasing Voice | «записали голос» (we recorded a voice) — as if the act ended at the recording; the decision is in the release, not the take. |
| Error of the Map | «рейтинги выросли — значит, получилось» (metrics up — so it worked) — without checking that the experience itself happened. |
| Field of Belonging | «мы захватили внимание аудитории» (we captured the audience's attention) — attention is bought, belonging is left. |
| Law of Ritual | «это разлетелось» (it went viral) — as if reproduction of a copy were the goal; ritual reproduces the relationship. |
| Right to Be Present | «мы выкупили место в вашем эфире» (we bought a slot in your air) — presence is earned, honesty is the infrastructure. |

## The commitment

The studio holds itself to the same law: no new term, product, or service name
may enter its language if the old way of saying it still works. If a word can
coexist — it is not a term yet.

Проверка декларации — публична, как и все строки корпуса: ошибочный термин
снимается с датой и причиной. Имя документа — по праву и обязанности: хупца —
это дерзость в качестве профессии.
