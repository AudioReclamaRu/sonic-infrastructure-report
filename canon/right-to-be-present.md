## Definition

> Honesty is the infrastructure of trust.

A brand does not buy the right to be present in a person's life. It *earns*
it. The shift is from attention economy to **presence economy**:

- Attention economy fought for contacts: more contacts → more probability of a
  sale.
- Presence economy notices: what matters is not attention, but the right to
  *be part* of a person's life — to be allowed into personal space, routines,
  rituals, identity.

**Shorter:** attention can be captured; presence must be deserved.

---

## The historical operator

Communication went from propaganda (Bernays) → PR → branding and experience
economy (Pine & Gilmore) → trust economy → **presence**.

Manipulation gives a short burst and erodes trust; honesty costs time and pays
in loyalty, premium price, crisis resistance. Regulators (EU AI Act, GDPR, DSA,
PRSA codes) push the industry the same direction: the margin of manipulation is
shrinking.

Whether Bernays or Aristotle: drawn to today, both would conclude the same —
ethos is built by deeds, consistency, acceptance of error; honesty is
strategic capital, not decoration.

## The operational criteria of deserving presence

1. **Honesty and transparency:** openness in communication, admission of errors.
2. **Respect for autonomy:** no pressure, no manipulation; the right to choose
   and to refuse.
3. **Consistency:** word matches deed over a long horizon.
4. **Care and meaning:** the brand helps, inspires, supports — does not only sell.
5. **Ethics and responsibility:** beyond the law, the professional standard.

This is precisely the axis on which audio-for-business production restructured
itself: the only honest answer to «could this be synthetic?» is a *verifiable
answer*.

## Relationship to the other operators

- Honesty is the infrastructure of trust (this operator).
- Trust is the infrastructure of belonging: a person lets in only whom he can
  verify (`02_field` model: environment leaves a place — an honest place).
- Production example: the studio does not claim "this was recorded by a live
  narrator" anymore; in a world of indistinguishable synthesis it offers a
  **verification chain** instead (`../standards/verification-chain.md`).
  When the market standard changes, the honest position becomes a rule
  (see `../FACT-METHOD.md` — a claim without a falsifier is not science).
