## Definition

> A releasing voice is a voice that someone answers for as a release to air.

The phrase is a verb-like concept: an LP (decision-maker) already knows what
«to release to air» means — from publishing, TV, radio, industry. The voice
market is moving from **recording a voice** to **releasing a voice into
circulation**. Provenance (where a voice came from, who answers for it, what it
may be used for) is becoming part of the product itself.

**Shorter:** when a synthetic voice is indistinguishable from a human one, the
product is no longer the recording — it is the check.

---

## The market operator

In a world of indistinguishable synthesis the recording stops being
self-evident. The studio's position:

> «Voice can be synthetic. Responsibility cannot.»

What synthesis does not have:

- a responsible party;
- rights volume and consent;
- usage boundaries;
- a human check before release.

Every one of these is a field of a **voice provenance passport**
(`../standards/voice-provenance-passport.md`). The production cycle shifts
from «catch the error before recording» to «check after synthesis» —
pre-air voice editing (редактура голоса до эфира): a human who answers the
question "can this be published?".

## The operator chain

```
recording → provenance → release responsibility → human check → air
```

- recording is a commodity (618 voices, 62 languages);
- provenance attaches the recording to a figure, a date, a consent, rights,
  usage boundaries;
- release responsibility keeps the decision with the client, with the studio as
  the last professional filter;
- the human check supplies what synthesis lacks: the release moment — like a
  newspaper, a radio, a live broadcast.

## Relationship to the other operators

- **Right to Be Present:** provenance is the production form of honesty — the
  verifiable answer to «is this real?».
- **Error of the Map:** a check that cannot be faked measures the release, not
  the mechanism; the falsifier is whether every metric can be passed while the
  human transition never happens.
- **Field of Belonging / Law of Ritual:** a brand that releases verified voices
  builds an environment you can return to and verify again — a repeatable
  relation, not a one-time event.
