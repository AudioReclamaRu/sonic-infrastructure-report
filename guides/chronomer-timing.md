# Chronomer: time your text before you order

**Type:** guide · **Audience:** enterprise / media agencies · **Last updated:** 2026-09-11
**Status:** live · **Language of record:** EN

A free online tool counts how long a text will sound when voiced — per character,
per word, at a chosen pace. The worth of the tool is not the count. It is that
a client comes to the order with a measured script: minutes are known before
the studio is involved.

## Why it matters

- Script length decides format (30s, 60s, IVR greeting, video or commercial).
- A measured brief reduces re-work and makes the estimate concrete.
- Freedom of use: it is the client's own tool, no account required.

## Live route

- The calculator: https://www.audio-reclama.ru/chronomer-stranitcemer.html
- Order with the measured script: https://www.audio-reclama.ru/online-zakaz.html

## Contract criterion

First count, then order. A script measured by the client is a script the
studio can quote without guessing — and the client can check the quote.
