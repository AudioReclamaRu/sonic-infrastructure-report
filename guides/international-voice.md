# International voice: you buy an obligation with an address

**Type:** guide · **Audience:** enterprise (global, localization) · **Last updated:** 2026-09-11
**Status:** live · **Language of record:** EN

For an international buyer a voice is not a performance. It is an obligation:
a recording rights-clear for the target markets, with a party named in the
contract that answers for it. The studio site runs an English surface under
/en/ and a product registration at audio-reclama.com.

## The obligation list

1. The recording is yours within the agreed term and territory.
2. Consent and rights of the performer are documented (standards/voice-provenance-passport.md).
3. Meaning is verified per market, not just translated (guides/airport-announcements.md).
4. The responsible party is named — where a claim is addressed.
5. The final version is frozen and verifiable (standards/verification-chain.md).

## Contract criterion

Do not buy a voice; buy an obligation with an address. If the contract does
not name the side that answers for the recording, the price has no content.
