# Video and presentation voiceover: who holds the final version

**Type:** guide · **Audience:** product / content / marketing · **Last updated:** 2026-09-11
**Status:** live · **Language of record:** EN

Video dubbing is now produced without an actor in a studio (Deepdub, Flawless).
What is not automated is the human release: checking figures, wording, stress,
rights, and freezing the final version before it is published.

## The release list for a video asset

1. Figures and on-screen numbers match the audio.
2. Wording is exact — no paraphrase of contractual or product phrases.
3. Stress and intonation survive the cut.
4. Rights cover every use the video will have.
5. The final mix is documented (standards/verification-chain.md).
