# Airport announcements: translation or meaning?

**Type:** guide · **Audience:** enterprise (transport, hubs, airports) · **Last updated:** 2026-09-11
**Status:** live · **Language of record:** EN

A multilingual hub does not buy «announcements in N languages». It buys a
guarantee that the meaning survives in each language. The question: who is
responsible for meaning on every language version, and who checks it before air?

## Why translation is not enough

- Numbers, gates, times, flight numbers — a mistranslated digit changes the
  passenger's decision.
- Localization must fit the host country's law and the hub's own announcements
  style.
- Each language version is an independent release: figures, wording, stress,
  rights, final version (standards/responsible-release.md).

## The check before air

- Who answers for the meaning on each language?
- Were the translations verified against the recording, not against the script?
- Is the final per-language version frozen and documented?
