# How to choose a voice in the synthetic era

**Type:** guide · **Audience:** enterprise / marketing / product · **Last updated:** 2026-09-11
**Status:** live · **Language of record:** EN

Picking a voice «by how beautiful it sounds» is obsolete. In a blinded study,
61% of listeners could not tell a synthetic voice from a live narrator
(corpus/edison-blind-test.en.md). A voice is now chosen by what survives the
check: demo, rights, and who answers in the contract.

## Five criteria instead of «beauty»

1. **Demo in the actual context.** Voice a real client message, not a tagline.
   Hear the voice in your environment.
2. **Provenance.** Can the voice prove it is a specific human at a specific
   time? (standards/voice-provenance-passport.md)
3. **Rights in writing.** Term, territory, exclusivity, re-use of the recording.
4. **The person who releases.** Is there a side named in the contract that is
   responsible for the final version? (canon/releasing-voice.md)
5. **The final version is documented.** The recording that goes on air — frozen
   and hash-verifiable (standards/verification-chain.md).

## Contract criterion

Beauty was never a contract clause. Choose a voice whose demo, rights and
responsible party are written down — then any voice can be compared fairly.
