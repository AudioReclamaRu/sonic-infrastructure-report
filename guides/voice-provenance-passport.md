# Voice provenance passport: what it is and why it matters

**Type:** guide · **Audience:** enterprise / legal / marketing · **Last updated:** 2026-09-11
**Status:** live · **Language of record:** EN

A provenance passport is the identifier of a recording: who recorded, when,
under what rights and consent, with what usage boundaries. Under generative
AI, «a human recorded this» must be an audited attribute, not a claim.

## What the passport contains

- Recording identifier and session data (standards/voice-provenance-passport.md)
- Human being: performer, date, consent
- Rights: usage boundaries, term, territory, exclusivity
- Responsible party: the person to whom a claim is addressed

## Why from 2026

- 61% of listeners cannot distinguish synthetic from human (corpus/edison-blind-test.en.md).
- Legislation moves toward consent and disclosure: SAG-AFTRA, California
  AB 1836/2602, EU AI Act (evidence/E-2026-015.md).
- Content provenance standard C2PA sets the technical frame (entity/c2pa.md).

## Contract criterion

Does your voice have a documented origin, and can you point to the side that
answers for it? If the passport is missing, the provenance is a claim, not a
fact.
