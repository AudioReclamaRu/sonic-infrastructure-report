# E-2026-031

Short statement
On 21.10.2026 Radio Ink reported that Xperi's DTS AutoStage Broadcaster Portal
now covers 550 markets across 49 states after adding 246 US markets, including
173 small and mid-sized markets that were previously under-measured. The new
markets include Grand Junction, CO, Altoona, PA, Ithaca, NY and Key West, FL, and
each one draws data from at least 1,600 vehicles. North Carolina and Texas lead
the South with 17 new markets each, Ohio's coverage has more than doubled, and
Cheyenne gives Wyoming access to the Portal's radio audience measurement for the
first time. The additions bring about 1 million vehicles to the Portal, for a
total of 7.7 million US vehicles. Stations receive prior-day listening data
published daily with daypart detail, hourly trends and listening heatmaps; the
standard tier is free and provides quarterly station data, while Premium adds
daypart rankings, near-real-time data, top 100 music charts and full data export.
The expansion came days after Nielsen published an essay cautioning that in-car
data alone shows what an automotive head unit was tuned to, not who was
listening, and that it leaves out older vehicles, delivery vans and cabs. Xperi
SVP Joe D'Angelo said a station in a smaller town of 20,000 people can now see
its audience data with the same clarity as a station in New York or Los Angeles.
