# E-2026-080

Short statement
On 27.11.2026 RadioToday reported that Cheshire's Mix 56 had been awarded £20,000 by The National Lottery Community Fund to take its mobile radio workshop into schools across the county. The station's Radio Roadshow Workshop lets pupils present behind a microphone, write news and travel bulletins and record audio, and introduces production roles behind a live radio programme. The project follows a session at Sir Thomas Boteler High School in Warrington, where the station says more than 60 young people took part during an open evening. Mix 56 is a not-for-profit local station based in Lymm; its weekly Kids Live at 5 programme lets children research, write and present material. Station director Paul Smith said the grant would help the station take the experience into more schools and give pupils a chance to build confidence and discover new skills (direct read 28.11.2026).

What it changes
- Gives a local station a funded mobile workshop to scale hands-on youth broadcasting from a single school event to schools across Cheshire, expanding the pipeline of young people who can speak, write and produce audio before they reach a professional studio.
