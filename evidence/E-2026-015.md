# E-2026-015

Short statement
Legal layer for synthetic voice (in force): California AB 1836 and AB 2602
(effective 17.09.2024) treat voice and digital likeness as objects of consent;
SAG-AFTRA TV/Theatrical 2023 ratified 78.33% toward regulating digital voice
copies; EU AI Act (2024/1689) is the regulatory framework for synthetic content.
