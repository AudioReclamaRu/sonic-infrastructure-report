# E-2026-073 — Voice-catalog discoverability and semantic clarity

## Observation and method

- **Observed:** 2026-11-18
- **Google Search Console window:** 2026-08-15 through 2026-11-15 (Pacific Time)
- **Country:** `RUS` only
- **Search type:** Web
- **Property:** `https://www.audio-reclama.ru/`
- **Source:** first-party Google Search Console property connected to OpenSEO project `f868933a-0943-4722-b8f1-d54ceb5d34a9`
- **Technical crawl:** OpenSEO site audit `baad0d1f-7d0e-4669-b1a3-21ef97bb4169`, completed 2026-11-17; 50 pages crawled out of a 50-page budget, while 1,077 URLs were discovered.
- **Scope:** Search Console data is a Russia-only search-performance cut. The crawl is a limited technical sample, not a full-site audit. No paid rank/keyword check or AI tracker was run.
- **Interpretation boundary:** search clicks, impressions and positions are not AI citations, enquiries, leads, sales or proof of recommendation by an AI system.

## Query evidence

### English-language voices (queries observed from Russia)

- `диктор по английскому языку`: 197 impressions, 32 clicks, position 1.6.
- `диктор английского языка`: 142 impressions, 28 clicks, position 1.7.
- `английский язык диктор`: 45 impressions, 11 clicks, position 1.2.
- `диктор на английском`: 194 impressions, 1 click, position 8.0.
- `американский диктор`: 7 impressions, 1 click, position 8.9 (male English subcategory).

The English catalogue page was crawled as indexable, HTTP 200, 2,058 words and 0 H1 headings; it listed a page heading in the visible body and displayed 86 English-language voices at the time of the public read. The dynamic count is a snapshot, not a stable claim. The official catalogue header states that the speaker catalogue covers 62 languages, but language/voice availability must still be checked for a concrete order.

### Known voices

The page received 5,816 impressions and 34 clicks. The broad query `дикторы` contributed 2,635 impressions, 4 clicks and average position 8.8; `актеры озвучки` contributed 573 impressions, 5 clicks and position 9.7.

This is a large attention surface with a low observed CTR. It merits a title/snippet and intent-fit review, but the query mix and aggregate data alone do not establish why users did not click. Do not change public claims about individual performers or imply endorsement without specific evidence and permissions.

## Semantic/crawl findings on catalogue pages

The 2026-11-17 50-page crawl reported:
- **7 catalogue/category pages with no H1:** known/character voices, children's voices, Arabic, English, German, male Russian and female Russian pages.
- **2 duplicated meta descriptions:** male Russian and female Russian catalogue pages share the same description.
- **Missing alt attributes:** the crawl reported 101/101 images missing alt on male voices, 101/101 on female voices and 101/101 on English voices. This count does not prove that all images are meaningful portraits; decorative images should retain empty alt text, while meaningful performer portraits should have accurate, contextual alternative text.
- Both male and female pages were crawled as HTTP 200 and indexable, with 2,113 and 2,128 words respectively. The crawl reported response times of 4,828 ms for male and 1,238 ms for female on that single crawl; this is a point observation, not an availability or Core Web Vitals test.

The two category pages expose different topics in their titles and visible page headings, but the crawler found no H1:
- Male page title: `Мужские русские дикторы. Лучшие дикторы России`
- Female page title: `Женские русские дикторы. Лучшие дикторы России`

A title/H1 should describe the actual filtered catalogue, not add unsupported "best", "native speaker", celebrity, rate, availability or quality guarantees. Before using a description, verify that the listed formats correspond to the individual page's actual scope.

## Priority and implications for AI discovery

1. **First semantic repair candidate:** add one accurate H1 to the seven existing catalogue/category pages; do not create new category URLs.
2. **Second:** make the male and female meta descriptions distinct and faithful to each page's actual content. Do not copy the generic description onto both.
3. **Third:** provide useful alt text to meaningful speaker portraits where the person/name is clearly associated with the image. Leave decorative imagery empty; never infer identity from a photo or invent a performer's credentials.
4. **Fourth:** review the title/snippet and intent fit of the "known voices" page because it attracts substantially more impressions than the other catalogue pages but has a low CTR in this period.
5. **Fifth:** preserve the English voice catalogue as a high-performing existing route. Do not hardcode dynamic voice counts into static copy or metadata; recheck availability and performer language for an individual order.

These changes may improve page-topic clarity, accessibility and retrieval consistency; no AI score lift or ranking gain is measured or guaranteed.
