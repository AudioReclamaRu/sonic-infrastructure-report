# E-2026-073

Short statement
Budget 2026 (Government of Canada, released 05.11.2026) proposes to provide an additional $300 million over two years, starting in 2026-27, to CBC/Radio-Canada (Canada's national public broadcaster) to strengthen its capacity to meet the evolving needs of Canadians across the country. The measure is stated in Budget 2026 Chapter 4 and in the Budget 2026: Key Measures backgrounder.

What it changes
- Confirms a federal budget commitment of $300 million over two years (starting 2026-27) for CBC/Radio-Canada's capacity, providing additional multi-year public funding support for Canada's national public broadcaster (including its radio and audio services) in Budget 2026.
