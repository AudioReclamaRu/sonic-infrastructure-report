# E-2026-077 — Third-party discovery surfaces with free profile routes

## Purpose and scope

This evidence record evaluates external, independently operated profile and directory surfaces that can create additional public, machine-readable representations of **Аудио-Реклама•ru** outside its own website.

This is an AI-discoverability opportunity audit, not an SEO/page-metadata audit. The goal is to increase the number and quality of independent sources that can describe the studio consistently and can be encountered by search/retrieval systems. A directory listing is not proof that an AI model will cite the company; that must be measured in real answers.

**Observed:** 19.11.2026  
**Method:** read public official listing/pricing/help pages and current directory category pages.  
**Actions:** no accounts created, no forms submitted, no profile claimed or edited, no outreach sent, no reviews requested, no case studies uploaded, no paid features selected.

## 1. Clutch — highest-value B2B directory candidate

Official sources:
- Get listed: https://clutch.co/get-listed
- Profile setup instructions: https://help.clutch.co/en/knowledge/get-listed-on-clutch
- Free features: https://help.clutch.co/en/knowledge/free-features-clutch-offers
- Enhanced features: https://help.clutch.co/en/knowledge/what-are-the-enhanced-profile-features-available-on-clutch
- Voiceover category: https://clutch.co/agencies/video-production/voiceovers
- Methodology: https://clutch.co/methodology

Verified terms and surfaces:
- A Basic company profile is explicitly **free**.
- Basic profile can appear on service-line directory pages, collect verified client reviews, include portfolio material, and publish up to three shareable case studies.
- Clutch's help page says a free profile is also included on its sister site **The Manifest**. This gives a potential second independent company-profile surface from one profile, subject to Clutch's review and publishing.
- Clutch manually reviews submitted profiles and says it accepts most business service providers with a functional website and verifiable full-time employees.
- Setup requires company name, tagline, number of employees, minimum project size, average hourly rate, website, location(s), contact details, company overview and service focus.
- Clutch has an active global “Top Voiceover Companies” directory. The page describes buyer discovery based on client reviews, portfolio samples and market data; its update timestamp varies across cached views. The page itself says some placements may earn a fee.
- Clutch's free Basic tier does **not** include a website link from directory pages and does not rank above non-verified providers.
- Clutch Verified is shown at **$499/year** and includes, among other features, enhanced directory placement, a website link from directory pages, and a profile-level “AI Visibility” feature. Advertiser options are separately priced.

AI-discoverability interpretation:
- This is the strongest broad B2B profile candidate because its voiceover category and provider pages are public, its profile has a structured company/service schema, and its free profile extends to The Manifest.
- A free listing can establish another public entity node even though it is not the highest-ranked entry and has no directory-page website backlink.
- A paid “AI Visibility” label on Clutch is a feature of the Clutch Verified product; it is not evidence of visibility in ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity and is not required for a free profile.
- No payment is recommended or authorized. Do not buy the $499/year Verified tier, advertising, or sponsorship.

Eligibility uncertainty:
- Clutch requests a verifiable full-time employee count, minimum project size and hourly rate. These must not be borrowed from Ruward, AllAdvertising or estimates; the owner must confirm actual, current values.
- Do not invent an office location or legal entity. Profile location fields must match the actual entity being represented.
- Only genuine client references with permission may be invited to submit reviews. No reviews may be fabricated, incentivized or written by the company as if they were clients.

## 2. Sortlist — strong free Audio Production category

Official sources:
- Provider registration terms: https://help.sortlist.com/en/articles/1714392-can-i-register-to-sortlist-listing-conditions
- Listing instructions: https://help.sortlist.com/en/articles/1714404-how-to-be-listed-as-a-provider-on-sortlist
- Pricing: https://www.sortlist.co.uk/providers/pricing
- Provider registration: https://www.sortlist.com/providers

Verified terms:
- Creating a provider/agency profile is free. The provider terms explicitly allow freelancers and one-person firms if accurately represented.
- The permitted service taxonomy includes **Audio Production**, making the business category fit direct rather than inferred.
- Sortlist says its marketing focus is currently Benelux, France and Spain, but it works with providers globally.
- The free profile can showcase works and reviews and be included in relevant directories.
- Sortlist+ is displayed at **£129/month** and provides paid promotion, a badge, a backlink, visibility/opportunity modules and credits. These are not necessary to create a free profile.
- The provider workflow asks for phone, website, email, services, headquarters, other offices, target/prospecting markets, core services, and minimum/maximum project budget per service.

AI-discoverability interpretation:
- Strong free category fit for Audio Production, particularly for European/B2B discovery. It creates a public, service-specific third-party company profile and could become a source for retrieval systems.
- The platform’s current market focus makes it more relevant to European/B2B discovery than a generic global directory, but it does not prove the company will receive leads or AI citations.
- No Sortlist+ subscription, paid lead credits or opportunity applications are authorized.

Eligibility uncertainty:
- Headquarters, target markets and project-budget ranges require owner confirmation. Do not set the Netherlands as company headquarters from the user's current location.
- Do not choose a target geography based on the user's location rather than the business's actual commercial availability.
- Case-study assets and reviews should be used only when facts and publication rights are confirmed.

## 3. Voice Over Resource Guide (VORG) — high topical relevance

Official sources:
- Free listing page: https://voiceoverresourceguide.com/get-your-free-listing/
- Main directory: https://voiceoverresourceguide.com/
- Regional directory: https://voiceoverresourceguide.com/regionals-homepage/
- Advertising page: https://voiceoverresourceguide.com/advertise-with-us/
- About: https://voiceoverresourceguide.com/about/

Verified terms:
- The official page explicitly says standard listings and updates are free; advertising and sponsorship are separate.
- It welcomes professional recording studios and audio-production facilities.
- It provides a Global/Remote location category. The page states that a physical address is required only to appear in Map Search.
- Submissions are human-reviewed; a submission does not guarantee publication.
- Its public materials describe the guide as an industry publication dating to 1988. That age is the publisher's own historical claim, not an independent traffic or citation metric.
- Paid annual sponsorship tiers exist, but no sponsorship is needed for a standard profile.

AI-discoverability interpretation:
- Strongest niche topical fit of the four surfaces: the directory is explicitly about voiceover resources and has a dedicated recording-studio category.
- A properly completed global/remote company listing would provide a third-party reference that describes the studio as an industry resource, separate from the company's own site.
- The physical-address/Map Search enhancement is not required for a standard remote/global listing. Do not invent an address to gain map placement.

Eligibility uncertainty:
- The listing form requires a business/service name, category, market/location and description; owner should confirm whether Global/Remote accurately represents delivery capability.
- The current site states a multi-country voice talent network, but that is not sufficient by itself to promise remote production in every country or language. Confirm the exact service reach before submission.
- Human approval and inclusion timing are outside the company's control.

## 4. The VoiceOver Community — relevant secondary directory

Official source: https://www.thevoiceovercommunity.com/resource-directory/

Verified terms:
- The publisher describes it as an international voiceover-industry resource directory.
- The directory currently displays 456 records, including 149 under Production Services / Recording Studios in the fetched snapshot.
- It offers a standard free listing and a paid enhanced listing.
- The platform explicitly warns that its directory cannot guarantee listing accuracy or completeness; buyers should verify providers directly.

AI-discoverability interpretation:
- A relevant topical directory citation opportunity, particularly for voiceover production and recording-studio discovery.
- Lower priority than Clutch, Sortlist and VORG because the audience is primarily voiceover professionals and the directory emphasizes resources rather than general B2B procurement.
- Use only the free standard listing. No enhanced listing is required.

Eligibility uncertainty:
- The directory is geographically organized and the current result set is distance-sorted around New York. Confirm the form's international/remote handling before submitting; do not create an inaccurate physical location.

## 7. No-cost strategy and release conditions

The high-value first wave is **Clutch Basic + The Manifest, Sortlist Free, and VORG Free**. The VoiceOver Community is a secondary specialist directory.

Before any profile is actually submitted:
1. Verify that the business is not already listed under a slightly different name or domain.
2. Confirm the correct legal/business entity and headquarters; do not use a third-party address estimate.
3. Confirm employee count, minimum project size, average rate and service budgets wherever the platform requests them.
4. Use the factual copy in research/AI_DISCOVERABILITY_EXTERNAL_PROFILE_COPY_2026-11-19.md.
5. Use only published project pages and permission-cleared materials. Do not create testimonials, client reviews, project KPIs or fabricated case-study outcomes.
6. Choose free/basic only. Stop before any paid subscription, verified badge, sponsor placement, lead-credit purchase, premium service or annual commitment.
7. Submit only after explicit owner authorization; this record itself does not authorize external forms, profile claims, messages or submissions.
