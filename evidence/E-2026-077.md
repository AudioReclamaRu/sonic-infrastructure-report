# E-2026-077

Short statement
On 18 November 2026 Australian broadcaster ARN announced at its Upfront27 event new audience targeting and campaign measurement tools. As reported by RedTech (citing ARN and quoting Angelo Sinibaldi, ARN director of data product strategy and commercialization), ARN is extending its partnership with Westpac DataX to expand sales uplift reporting beyond digital audio to include national linear radio campaigns, using credit card transaction data from more than 13 million Westpac customers (described by ARN as an Australian audio market first). ARN also said it plans to launch iHeart Nation in 2027, a lifestyle segmentation product drawing on first-party data across its ecosystem (including more than 5 million registered iHeart users, podcast listening, radio streaming across more than 1,000 stations, music streaming and promotional interactions), and is extending its Databricks partnership to adopt CustomerLake.

What it changes
- Adds announced/expanded capabilities linking national linear radio campaigns to sales outcomes and building first-party audience targeting in Australian audio/radio, affecting ad measurement and monetisation infrastructure (with iHeart Nation described as planned for 2027).

Verification limits
- Based on direct read of the RedTech report on 20.11.2026 (including ARN statements/quotes). ARN's primary announcement was not separately accessed in this session; details and claims (including "Australian audio market first") are stated as reported. Planned elements (iHeart Nation in 2027) are not yet implemented.
