# E-2026-017

Short statement
On 04.09.2026 Samuel Fitoussi published the argument that it is rational to
prefer human-written content over AI-generated content of similar or better
quality. Four reasons: attention selection (publishing costs forced authors
to select ideas; LLM publishers face no selection cost), noise inserted
between the author's mental state and the reader, the broken implicit
contract of effort ("you do not feel received"), and "we already have
chatbots". The author states the logic transfers beyond writing; it applies
to voice.
