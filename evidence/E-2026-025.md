# E-2026-025

Short statement
On 19.10.2026 Radio Ink published Sounds Profitable's "Super Spenders" research
(based on the Podcast Atlas, fielded by Signal Hill among 5,061 US adults and
weighted to the census): super spenders are podcast consumers who spent more than
$300 on podcast ad-driven purchases in the past three months. Nearly 40% of monthly
podcast consumers made a podcast-driven purchase in that window, about 28% crossed
the $300 line, and that group accounts for roughly 62% of tracked podcast-driven
spending. Super spenders consumed 6.8 genres in the past 30 days versus about five
for the average podcast consumer, and a host read in the host's own words is the
most persuasive ad format for both super spenders and moderate spenders. An earlier
Podcast Atlas finding cited in the same article: only 31% of audio podcast consumers
question ad claims most or all of the time.
