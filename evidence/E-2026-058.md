# E-2026-058

Short statement
On 17.11.2026 Radio Ink reported that the Premiere Networks syndicated Johnjay and Rich Show held its ninth #LovePup Family Fest, drawing more than 8,000 people to Vertuccio Farms in Mesa, Arizona, on Saturday 7 November and finding forever homes for 70 dogs. Gavin DeGraw headlined the free concert, with sets from The Rich Berra Band and magician Geno Ploeger. Johnjay and Blake Van Es founded the #LovePup Foundation as an Arizona nonprofit; the fest will add Austin and San Antonio in 2027.
