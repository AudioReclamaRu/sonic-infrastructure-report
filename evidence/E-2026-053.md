# E-2026-053

Short statement
On 17.11.2026 Radio Ink reported that David Hoffmann, who agreed in June to pay $20 million for six St. Louis stations including KMOX, made an unsolicited offer to buy all 342 Townsquare Media stations for $17 million more, roughly $37 million for the whole company. The letter proposed $2.03 per share in cash and put enterprise value near $490 million against a market capitalization of about $25 million after the stock lost nearly three-quarters of its value this year. Townsquare CEO Bill Wilson said the board expects to reject the offer after review.
