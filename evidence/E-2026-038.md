# E-2026-038

Short statement
On 04.11.2026 radioink.com reported: Political advertising lifted Townsquare Media’s third quarter, but it could not hide the pressures local clients are feeling. With costs and interest rates up, the company is leaning on programmatic growth and new partner markets while bracing for a tough Q4. Townsquare reported third-quarter net revenue of $110.9 million, up 3.9% year-over-year and 1.4% excluding political, above its guidance range of $108 million to $110 million. Broadcast advertising net revenue rose 0.4% to $47.2 million and fell 5.2% to $44.4 million excluding political, which totaled $2.9 million company-wide in the quarter. The Media Partnerships business now serves 18 partners and extends Townsquare’s digital programmatic platform to 122 markets across the United States, up from 16 partners and 115 markets in the second quarter. Digital Advertising net revenue grew 12.7% to $45.3 million, accelerating for the third consecutive quarter, with programmatic revenue up 22.5%.
