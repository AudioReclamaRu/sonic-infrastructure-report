# E-2026-044

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 Voice Over Herald reported that Tobias Meister, the German voice of Brad Pitt, Yoda and Robert Downey Jr.'s Tony Stark, sued The Walt Disney Company (Germany), the company's Munich subsidiary, in Munich for a bigger share of the money his films made. On Monday, 16 November, the Munich Regional Court I heard the claim covering twelve films Meister dubbed or wrote German dialogue for; by the afternoon the court and Meister's lawyer, Nikolaus Reber, announced a settlement and nobody would say what it was worth. Meister, 69, has been Brad Pitt's German voice since Seven in 1995, took over Yoda with the Star Wars prequels, and spoke for Downey's Tony Stark from the first Iron Man in 2008 through Avengers: Endgame. The case rests on section 32a of Germany's copyright act, the "fairness paragraph", which lets a creative contributor ask for more when the original fee is wildly out of line with what the work earned. Reber told Bild that Endgame alone drew more than 5 million cinemagoers in Germany and took around 42 million euros, while Bild reported that Meister was paid 20,000 euros. The opening demand was around 300,000 euros and Reber said it could grow to as much as 1.5 million. Disney argued that parts of the claim were time barred. Meister told Bild he no longer gets any engagements at Disney, and another actor will dub Downey as Victor von Doom in Avengers: Doomsday, which opens in German cinemas on 16 December.
