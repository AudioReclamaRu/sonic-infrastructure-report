### Expert-request and industry-directory platforms

4. **Pressfeed**:

   - The public free Start plan shows 0 ₽, access to requests delayed by up to 3 hours, and 1 answer per month.
   - Direct searches for audio/voice/advertising/podcast requests surfaced several relevant-looking request pages, including a soft-podcast guest call, a podcast case-library call, and an invitation to call-centre/mailing-service representatives.    - A free account is a low-cost discovery/response route, but the current search sample does not establish that a suitable live request is available now.

5. **Podcastbery**:
   - Partner page: https://podcastbery.ru/connect
   - Directory home: https://podcastbery.ru/
   - The platform describes itself as a searchable booking directory for podcast, recording and video studios; its home page displayed 224 studios at the time of the live read.
   - The partner page claims 1,000+ monthly visitors. This is the platform's own claim and was not independently verified.
   - Published partner path: application → manager contact → contract → partner account / studio profile.
   - The page lists STANDART, PRO and PREMIUM tiers; states 20% from an order; and offers a seven-day trial for PRO and PREMIUM. Exact fixed subscription prices and the detailed mapping of commission/benefits by tier were not visible in the page content retrieved.

The presence of an office address or distributed narrator recording network does not prove that a studio is available for third-party rental.

   - This route is classified as paid / not activated, not as a free acquisition opportunity.
