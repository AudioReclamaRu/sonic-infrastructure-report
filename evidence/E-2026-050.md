# E-2026-050

Short statement
On 17.11.2026 Radio Ink reported that public radio outlet The SoCal Sound gave its Orange County 88.5 FM signal its own call letters, changing them from KSBR to KJOC. The change separates the Orange County broadcast from the KCSN-HD2 simulcast in Los Angeles and the San Fernando Valley, while the station’s on-air brand remains 88.5 The SoCal Sound. According to the report, the new call letters KJOC are a nod to the station’s focus on Orange County, with “JOC” standing for “Just Orange County.” The move gives the Orange County service a distinct broadcast identity while maintaining the existing on-air name across the simulcast.
