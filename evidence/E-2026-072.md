# E-2026-072

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 Médiář reported that Radiohouse, the sales agency of the Media Bohemia group, will from 1 January 2027 sell advertising in digital radio regionally for the first time and will launch a new offer called "Pure DAB+ Pack". The pack is to include stations broadcast only in DAB+ (Cool DAB+, WorldRadio, Energic, ZUN rádio, Radio 7 and Rádio B) and will allow advertisers to target campaigns regionally. The report linked the move to the start of 2027 DAB+ network changes in the Czech Republic and to regionalised DAB+ broadcasting developments.

What it changes
- Introduces a planned commercial product for regional ad sales and a DAB+-only station package in the Czech market from 01.01.2027, indicating a monetization/market-structuring use of regional DAB+ distribution.

Verification limits
- Based on direct read of the Médiář report on 19.11.2026 and WorldDAB newsroom relay (16.11.2026). Commercial terms, actual campaign uptake, and effects beyond what the sources state are not independently verified in this note. This is a separate development from E-2026-068 (technical regional switching capability for RockZone/Český Impuls activated 12.11.2026).
