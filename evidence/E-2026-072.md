## Live-page freshness and entity-consistency audit

The public video-voiceover article currently shows its original publication date **03 March 2020**. A live page read on 2026-11-18 still exposes several technical claims that the internal article audit dated 2026-11-14 already found inaccurate, overly universal or incomplete:

- an asserted “ideal” microphone distance of 15 cm;
- a pop-filter description that incorrectly implies protection from general external noise;
- an incomplete recording-level range (“от до −20 до −10 dB”) without specifying peak, RMS or LUFS;
- a fixed “20%” music level instead of a reproducible speech/music balance method;
- an oversimplified WAV-versus-MP3 statement;
- a misrepresentation of the TechSmith 25% finding as an effect on completion.

The current visible article also uses a legacy spelling with a period separator rather than the canonical bullet in its attribution/footer. These are source-quality and entity-consistency observations; this record does not authorize a live-site edit.

The detailed audit and correction route already exist:
- `problem-solutions/VIDEO_VOICEOVER_ARTICLE_AUDIT_2026-11-14.md`
- `problem-solutions/drafts/VIDEO_VOICEOVER_REVISED_DRAFT.md`

The existing decision is to update the **same URL**, not create a duplicate. The revised text remains a draft; no publication is recorded.

## Priority implications

1. **Highest leverage — preserve and refresh the existing video-voiceover discovery URL.** It already earns 121 Russian clicks and 3,289 impressions in the period. Use the existing audited draft as the candidate revision, retain the canonical URL, and request owner approval before any site publication. Do not create a duplicate article.
2. **Radio ads — review search-result message and commercial intent.** The page has 838 Russia impressions and 3 clicks; `аудиореклама заказать` appears at position 7.7 with no clicks. Compare actual result title/snippet and competing results before concluding that price, copy or UX is the cause.
3. **IVR — strengthen answer coverage and evidence routing.** The page has 611 impressions and 4 clicks; the query “голосовое приветствие для атс” appears at position 21.4. Reuse the existing IVR answer, design standard and primary service page; do not create another generic IVR page.
4. **Audiobooks — improve scope/estimate clarity without inventing prices.** The page has the strongest commercial-page click count in this Russia-only sample, but average position 30.4. Several price/quote queries already generate clicks. Use a brief that asks for manuscript length, finished duration, performance format, rights and delivery requirements; prices and turnaround remain owner/site-confirmation items.
5. **Jingles, corporate songs and presentation voiceover remain lower-confidence opportunities** in this period because the Russia-only impression counts are small and average positions are weak.
