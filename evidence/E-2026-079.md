# E-2026-079

Short statement
On 11.11.2026 Sounds Profitable published *AI and Podcast Consumers* (Tom Webster; data from *The Podcast Landscape 2026*, Signal Hill Insights), reporting that 10% of past-month AM/FM broadcast radio listeners said they had watched or listened to fully AI-generated content in that medium over the past month — the lowest share among 17 media measured; podcasts were 20% and TikTok 60%. Radio Ink reported the same figures on 19.11.2026 (direct read 22.11.2026).

What it changes
- Provides a measured audience-perception signal: AM/FM radio currently has lower reported exposure to fully AI-generated content than other measured media, while audiences remain sensitive to undisclosed/synthetic hosts. This frames radio as a relative trust/safe-harbor signal, but also raises the downside risk if synthetic AI audio appears in a medium where listeners do not expect it.

Verification limits
- Primary report page and Radio Ink coverage directly read on 22.11.2026. The Sounds Profitable PDF was downloaded but text extraction was not reliable in this session; figures used in publication are those explicitly reported by Radio Ink from the Sounds Profitable report and consistent with the report landing page description. Full methodology/sample details should be read from the original PDF before making stronger statistical claims.
