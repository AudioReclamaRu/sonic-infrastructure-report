# E-2026-069

Short statement
On 13.11.2026 SatelliFax reported that Luxembourg's national DAB+ ensemble (LUX-NATIONAL1, channel 7D) reached its provisional final rollout. With the commissioning of the Weiswampach transmitter (5 kW transmission power), coverage in the north of the Grand Duchy was improved. The network now consists of nine transmitter sites; the second expansion stage was completed with the Weiswampach launch. WorldDAB's newsroom item (16.11.2026) relayed the same milestone and linked to SatelliFax.

What it changes
- Marks completion of the provisional final expansion phase of Luxembourg's national DAB+ network, extending terrestrial digital radio coverage in the north and consolidating a nine-site national infrastructure.

Verification limits
