# E-2026-033

Short statement
On 30.10.2026 Radio Ink reported on a new IAB guide, "Amplifying Audio's Impact in
MMM", which argues that audio underperforms in media mix models because the data
reaching the model is too thin, not because the channel performs badly. The guide
cites a 2012 study for the radio industry run by Sequent Partners for Arbitron:
in three real-world remodels, replacing planned radio data with a record of ads
that actually aired raised measured radio return by 27 points to 78% with the model
structure otherwise untouched, and the model's confidence in the radio result rose
from 92% to 97%. Independent benchmarks place digital audio in the upper half of
channel returns, and Nielsen Compass figures rank broadcast radio fourth in the US.
A commercial product launched around 2018 combined electronic airing detection with
metered-panel audience estimates but covered 45 markets and excluded network radio,
and one holding company submitted as-run data on network radio models for roughly
two years without a material improvement. The guide traces eight hand-offs where
detail is lost — three that affect every channel, three that fall harder on audio and
two specific to audio — and notes that broadcast, streaming, podcasting and satellite
enter a model as one line. An agency-supplied analysis of one five-week campaign
across those four channels found cost per acquisition varied roughly threefold between
the cheapest and the most expensive platform, with satellite carrying a little over
half the spend; matching listeners by network address returned nothing for satellite
while the statistical method put it at the campaign's highest cost per acquisition.
The guide sets thresholds of about 50 million national impressions a year and at least
three to five local markets, lists required delivery-feed fields from date, publisher
and brand to impressions, spend and DMA-level geography, and notes that SiriusXM
Media's first-draft data dictionary has become a live feed of 21 fields. It also says
only the advertiser can change the model specification, because the advertiser
commissions and pays for it.
