# E-2026-075

Short statement
On 16 November 2026 Radio World reported that Malaysia's national public broadcaster Radio Television Malaysia (RTM) deployed a centralized radio asset and archive management system from CGI and Malaysian integrator Cedar Broadcast & Communications. RTM operates 34 radio stations nationwide from its Angkasapuri broadcast headquarters in Kuala Lumpur. Using CGI's dira platform, the system replaces RTM's legacy archive infrastructure, integrates across multiple sites with existing dira radio and news production clusters, and supports automated and manual archive and restore capabilities for music libraries, news material, show recordings and historical programs. The report states the disk-based archive supports MPEG, AAC, BWF and WAV formats, introduces an offline archive tier (high-resolution media migrated to a deep archive media asset manager while low-resolution browse copies and metadata remain accessible), and is expected to support more than 200 hours of archived content per month with music and library exports projected at approximately 500 hours monthly.

What it changes
- Modernises and centralises radio asset/archive infrastructure for a 34-station national public broadcaster in Malaysia, affecting production/news workflows and long-term preservation/access of radio content at national scale.
