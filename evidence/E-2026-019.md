# E-2026-019

Short statement
Share of Ear (Edison Research at SSRS, 01.07.2026): on-demand platforms took eight
years to tie linear audio (the crossing occurred in 2023); in 2026, 56% of time spent
with audio goes to on-demand platforms, the same 12-point margin linear audio held
in 2020, now in the opposite direction.

Source
- Primary: https://www.edisonresearch.com/time-spent-with-on-demand-audio-surpasses-linear-sources/
