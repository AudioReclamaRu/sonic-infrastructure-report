# E-2026-106 — Live metadata and demonstration inventory on commercial pages

## Method

Read-only extraction of public page metadata, extracted text and outbound links on 2026-11-18. The observation establishes what the extractor returned on that date. It does not establish how every browser, search engine or AI crawler renders the page, and it does not prove that omitted dynamic elements do not exist.

## Observations

### Advertising audio page

Primary source: https://www.audio-reclama.ru/audio/radioroliki-zakaz.html

- The title contains the concepts «заказать рекламный аудиоролик» and «цена изготовления».
- The description says to order a voiceover and start by listening to radio advertising examples; it does not state a price or a price-calculation method.
- Extracted body text lists scripts, voice casting, sound design, licensed music and transfer of rights; it lists portfolio categories and shows a telephone contact.
- The extracted page exposes **65 direct MP3 links** among 89 outbound links. This is a count of links returned by the extraction, not a count of independently verified playable or currently licensed works.
- No numeric tariff table appeared in the extracted body text. This is not proof that no price information is visible in every rendering or on a linked page.

### IVR and phone greetings page

Primary source: https://www.audio-reclama.ru/audio/golosovoe-privetstvie-menu-ivr.html

- The description states a starting price of **1,000 ₽** and a readiness period of **one day**, while also saying the work accounts for PBX, VoIP and mobile-operator characteristics.
- The extracted body contains greeting and menu examples, bilingual template messages, and contact/order routes; it does not expose a numeric tariff table in the returned body text.
- The extraction returned **30 direct MP3 links** among 53 outbound links. This is a link count, not a test of the files or proof that each is a current authorized case.

### Video voiceover page

Primary source: https://www.audio-reclama.ru/audio/ozvuchka-video.html

- The title includes an order and price intent.
- The description explains video voiceover and adaptation/translation but does not show a price.
- The extracted body describes four named examples (Avansa, IGLA, JokaSafe and SpeedFit) and provides contact/order information.
- No numeric rate table appeared in the extracted body text. This is not proof that no price information exists in other page elements or linked material.

### Homepage identity control

Primary source: https://www.audio-reclama.ru

- The homepage title and Open Graph site name returned the canonical brand spelling «Аудио-Реклама•ru».
- The extracted homepage body again displayed two simultaneous general voice counts, 622 and 620. The issue is already recorded separately in E-2026-097; do not normalize either number.

## What this supports

- Commercial snippets should be compared with the actual visible offer, not inferred from a title alone.
- The advertising and IVR pages expose substantial direct audio demonstration links, which are potential proof assets that can be highlighted more clearly in their buyer journey.
- Metadata claims and the extracted page body are distinct evidence fields and should not be treated as interchangeable.

## What this does not support

- It does not prove why Google users clicked or did not click.
- It does not establish the current validity of any price or delivery promise beyond the fact that the description displayed those words on the observation date.
- It does not independently verify customer identities, commercial outcomes, rights, sample playback or service quality.
- It does not authorize edits to the website.

**p = 1.0** applies only to the captured page metadata, extracted text and returned link counts on 2026-11-18.  
**Falsifier:** a reproducible fresh fetch or browser observation on the same date that returns different metadata, body content or link counts. If found, preserve the conflicting capture and update the record rather than silently replacing it.
