# E-2026-081 — X (Twitter): account discovery, Metricool connection cost and Russia-market fit

## Purpose

Evaluate whether connecting X to the existing publishing workflow is a sensible next step for «Аудио-Реклама•ru», with an explicit no-spend decision.

## 1. Existing company account and connected workflow

Observed 19 November 2026:
- The company owner supplied the existing profile URL: https://x.com/audioreclamaru (`@audioreclamaru`). The user identifies it as the company's account. The direct public profile fetch in this research session returned HTTP 403, so profile text, follower count, post history, audience geography and account controls were not independently inspected.
- The connected Metricool brand is `«Аудио-Реклама•ru»`, brand ID `8189992`, timezone `Europe/Moscow`.
- Metricool's live connected-network state lists Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. No X/Twitter network appears; the existing X profile is therefore not connected to this Metricool brand at the time of the check.
- The earlier exact-name/domain search miss was a research limitation, not evidence that no profile exists. The account URL was supplied directly by the owner in this conversation.
- No X account was created or claimed, no OAuth authorization was granted, and no post was scheduled or published during this review.

## 2. Metricool's current connection and publishing requirements

Official Metricool Help Center:
- X connection overview: https://help.metricool.com/how-to-connect-x-twitter-to-metricool-kw6ev
- X scheduling and publishing: https://help.metricool.com/schedule-and-publish-on-x-twitter-klho8
- X/Twitter add-on pricing: https://help.metricool.com/your-guide-to-the-x-twitter-add-on-wt5wy

Verified terms (current help pages, fetched 19 November 2026):
- X management in Metricool requires a paid Metricool plan (Starter or higher) plus the X/Twitter add-on.
- The current add-on is €10 / $10 per month per connected X account, or €120 / $120 per year.
- One add-on covers one X account per Metricool brand.
- Adding it takes effect immediately and charges the full or prorated amount.
- The connection flow requires the X account login and explicit OAuth authorization in Metricool; Metricool warns that the user account must be available for the connection.
- Metricool supports scheduling X posts. Standard posts are 280 characters; X Premium can support up to 25,000 characters. The Metricool help page says links count as 23 characters.
- Metricool's content composer supports text, up to four images, one video or one GIF, and thread composition. The exact post capability should still be checked against the current account and plan before use.

Decision:
- Do not purchase the add-on or upgrade the plan without explicit spend approval.
- Since the account itself has not been verified and Russian-market audience fit is weak, do not add the recurring fee at this stage.

## 3. Russia-market audience evidence

Independent source:
- DataReportal, Digital 2027: The Russian Federation: https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-russian-federation
- Relevant section: “X users in the Russian Federation in 2027”, published in November 2026 and based on X advertising resources for late 2026.

Observed metric:
- X advertising resources indicated a potential advertising audience of 549,000 in Russia in late 2026, equivalent to about 0.4% of the total population and 0.4% of the local internet-user base.

Critical metric caveats:
- DataReportal explicitly states these are advertising-reach figures, not monthly active users; the two can differ materially.
- X's own advertising planning data show short-term volatility, and DataReportal advises caution in interpreting changes.
- This evidence supports only the conclusion that X's ad-planning audience is relatively small in Russia. It does not prove actual organic active-user counts or the size of any specific B2B buyer segment.

Access context:
- An independent cross-country availability check on 16 November 2026 reported that x.com was blocked on some Russian networks but reachable on others. This is not an authoritative legal statement and is a network-level observation, not proof every Russian user is unable to access X.
- Do not use VPN spoofing or false country/account identity to evade geographic restrictions.

## 4. Automation policy and operational safeguards

X's official automation rules:
- https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation
- X allows automated broadcasting of helpful information through compliant tools.
- X warns against non-API automation such as scripting the website, spam, unsolicited messaging and abuse of rate limits.
- X states account owners remain responsible for third-party applications authorized to act on their accounts.

Operational implication:
- If X is adopted later, use a supported publisher with explicit user-granted permissions (e.g. Metricool), not browser macros, bots, automatic mass-following, unsolicited DMs or automated engagement loops.
- Content automation should mean scheduling original, relevant posts, not blindly copying every platform post at identical frequency. Keep human review of factual claims, rights and links.

## 5. Recommendation

**Keep the existing X profile as a valid branded distribution surface; defer paid Metricool integration pending an audience/value check and explicit spend approval.**

Reasons:
1. An existing profile URL, `https://x.com/audioreclamaru`, was supplied by the company owner; the previous search miss was not evidence of absence.
2. The profile itself was not independently inspectable in this session (public fetch returned 403), so its bio, activity, audience size and buyer relevance remain unknown.
3. The connected Metricool brand has no X/Twitter connection. Official Metricool terms require Starter or higher plus an X add-on at €10/$10 per month per connected account (€120/$120 yearly); adding it takes effect immediately and may generate a prorated charge. Whether a base-plan upgrade is also needed depends on the account's current plan, which was not available in the brand-settings response.
4. DataReportal's late-2026 Russian X advertising reach (549,000; 0.4% of population) is an ad-planning estimate, not MAU and not a measure of the account's B2B audience. It is not sufficient by itself to dismiss an existing account or decide its commercial value.

Next decision gate:
- Inspect the live profile and recent posts in X itself; record follower count, recent activity, profile completeness, outbound site link and relevant audience signals.
- Compare the profile's likely international and Russian buyer relevance with the incremental Metricool cost.
- Only connect through Metricool after the owner explicitly approves the exact add-on/base-plan spend and grants OAuth access.
- Until then, keep the profile as an existing public channel; no post or OAuth connection is made.

Never use non-API browser automation, mass-following, unsolicited DMs, spam or engagement loops. Do not assume native scheduling is available on this account without checking its actual X interface and plan.

## 6. Actions and restrictions

- No Metricool add-on or base-plan upgrade was purchased.
- No OAuth permission was granted and no posts were scheduled or published.
- No paid promotion, API subscription, campaign or engagement automation was used.
- No changes to the official website or existing social accounts were made.
