Short statement
On 04.11.2026 radioink.com reported: After decades of guiding broadcasters through FCC rules, a top US communications law firm has suddenly closed its doors. Clients of Fletcher Heald & Hildreth were notified late last week that the firm would cease operations as of November 1. The firm, which dates to 1935, served as legal counsel to more than 1,500 broadcast radio and television stations, state and local governments, and cable TV companies, among other clients, with practice areas spanning media, intellectual property, telecommunications, technical innovation, government relations, and internet law. A staple of Radio Ink events, he is set to moderate “Broadcast on the Docket: Power, Policy, and Pressure in Washington” at Forecast 2027 . Seth Williams, who joined FHH in November 2018 and previously clerked for former Acting FCC Chairwoman Mignon Clyburn and the FCC’s Audio Division, began work Monday at Scale LLP alongside colleague Adam Sandler. Sandler, a communications and media attorney with more than 20 years of experience who shares a name with the actor and comedian, joined FHH as Counsel in June .
