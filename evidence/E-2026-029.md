# E-2026-029

Short statement
On 29.10.2026 RAJAR published its Q3 2026 results for UK radio: total weekly listening
reached 51 million people, a 2% year-on-year increase and a new record for the
medium. Online listening rose to 30.8% of total listening from 29.7% in Q3 2025,
well ahead of AM/FM at 23.6%. Smart speakers account for 18.7% of all radio
listening. The BBC's combined market share fell from 42.6% to 41.9% while its weekly
audience stayed broadly stable at just over 31 million. BBC Local Radio in England
saw weekly reach decline from 4.690 million to 4.425 million (down 5.7%) while total
listening hours rose slightly from 32.5 million to 32.6 million. Nation Broadcasting
recorded a 38% increase in weekly reach and added over two million hours in a single
quarter.
