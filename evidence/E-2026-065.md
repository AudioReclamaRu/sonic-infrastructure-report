# E-2026-065

Short statement
On 17.11.2026 RadioToday reported that the Audio Academy, supported by Aiir, revealed its 2026 30 Under 30 cohort: 30 people under 30 working in UK radio, podcasting, audiobooks and music. The initiative is in its 16th year and has run since 2011; Aradhna Tayal Leach said the list spotlights people who go on to shape the industry. Those selected receive free Audio Academy membership for a year and tickets to the ARIAS and Audio Academy Festival.
