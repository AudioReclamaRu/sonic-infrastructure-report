# E-2026-051

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 TechCrunch reported that Jordan Belfort's voice AI platform Tone is opening a self-service service that turns books into expressive AI-generated narration, with a voice-cloning feature rolling out for authors. Belfort said the system uses about 15 tonalities built from 30 years of sales data, answers with roughly 600 milliseconds of latency, has generated about $700,000 in revenue and has raised about $10 million from friends, family and seed investors. Tone's customers include law firms, health insurance companies and polling and automotive clients; the company calls only opt-in leads.
