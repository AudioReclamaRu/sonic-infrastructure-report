# E-2026-035

Short statement
On 03.11.2026 Radio Ink reported that AM/FM radio held 81% of ad-supported audio
time in the car among persons 18 and older, according to Edison Research's Share
of Ear data for the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026,
while podcasts accounted for 9%, ad-supported SiriusXM 4%, ad-supported Spotify
3%, ad-supported Pandora 2% and ad-supported YouTube Music 0.6%. The figures were
presented by Cumulus Media's Westwood One Audio Active Group Chief Insights Officer
Pierre Bouvard from an Advertiser Perceptions survey of 304 marketers and agencies
conducted October 1 to 13, in which 59% commuted to work all or most days, down
from 64% in April, 32% commuted some days and 8% worked from home only, for a total
of 91% commuting in some fashion, up from 63% in April 2022. Commuters averaged 4.2
office days a week in October 2024 and November 2025, the same as in this survey,
and a Quantilope study of 1,600 adults 18 and older, also conducted in October,
found that 87% of pre-COVID commuters now work outside the home.
