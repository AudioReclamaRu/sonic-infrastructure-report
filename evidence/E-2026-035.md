# E-2026-035

Short statement
On 03.11.2026 Radio Ink published an analysis by Pierre Bouvard, Chief Insights Officer of
Cumulus Media's Westwood One Audio Active Group, built on two October surveys. An Advertiser
Perceptions survey of 304 marketers and agencies (fielded 1-13 October 2026) found that 59%
commute to work all or most days, down from 64% in April, while 32% commute some days and 8%
work from home only; those commuting in any fashion total 91%, up from 63% in April 2022.
Edison Research's Share of Ear data for the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter
of 2026 puts AM/FM radio at 81% of ad-supported audio time in the car among persons 18 and
older, with podcasts at 9%, ad-supported SiriusXM at 4%, ad-supported Spotify at 3%,
ad-supported Pandora at 2% and ad-supported YouTube Music at 0.6%. A Quantilope study of
1,600 adults 18 and older, also run in October, found that 87% of pre-COVID commuters now
work outside the home, against 91% of marketers and agencies; marketers and agencies who
commute average 4.2 days a week in the office, while average Americans log 4.7 days. Five
days a week in the office is the most common schedule for both groups, at 51%. Marketers are
most likely to be in the office on Tuesday and Wednesday (88% each), followed by Thursday
(86%), Monday (78%) and Friday (67%); for average Americans Wednesday leads at 84%, with
Monday, Tuesday and Thursday at 82% and Friday at 75%. Weekend work is more common among
Americans (25% on Saturday and 16% on Sunday) than among marketers and agencies (11% and
3%). In-person meetings with media vendors have resumed for 80% of marketers and agencies,
up from 41% in April 2022, and 81% have resumed in-person conferences and events, up from
33%. Radio Ink notes that commuting marketers averaged 4.4 office days a week in April, the
highest since tracking began in 2022, and that commuters averaged 4.2 days in October 2024
and November 2025, the same as in this survey.
