## What the traffic-growth mandate means

The assistant is expected to work beyond document checking or passive recommendations. The mandate includes researching and acting across appropriate acquisition routes: organic search and technical SEO, useful content and intent coverage, AI-search visibility, legitimate references and links, brand mentions, referral partnerships, Dzen and other suitable distribution surfaces, directories and profiles, audio/video integrations, experiments, measurement and conversion paths.

The objective is **real, relevant visits to Audio-Reclama.ru and the resulting enquiries**, not vanity numbers or the volume of repository commits. Public claims, commercial promises and partner representations must remain accurate. Automated fake visits, spam, deceptive cloaking, unauthorized placements, impersonation and fabricated endorsements are not valid growth.

The website's direct-edit and deployment permissions remain governed by the current owner-approved repository operating boundary; the traffic mandate by itself does not claim that a code deployment or website edit has been performed.

## What remains variable in distributed production

The following must be established for the actual order/session and must not be hard-coded as universal network constants:

- which local studio / recording node is used for the particular narrator and job;
- whether a narrator uses the same location for every project;
- the actual microphone, preamp, interface, computer and DAW chain;
- where the clean, unprocessed recording is physically captured and which device writes the source file;
- the connection used for direction, talkback and monitoring;
- the route, transport method and intermediate steps by which the source file reaches the production team;
- which engineer or operator performs each intermediate stage and where;
- which post-production steps are required for the particular deliverable.

This does **not** mean these elements change on every job. It means they must not be assumed constant unless the current assignment has been checked.

## Boundaries of the current confirmation

The owner's statements establish accountability and the identity of the final decision-maker. They do not yet define:

- the detailed final-QC checklist for each deliverable type;
- mandatory technical measurements or numerical pass/fail thresholds;
- how the review result is logged, versioned or linked to a final-file checksum;
- which supplied files, screenshots, reports or playback evidence are required for the owner to review;
- how urgent exceptions, re-recording, client approval or disputed results are escalated;
- the current baseline of website visits, organic clicks, referral sessions, leads or conversion rate.

Do not invent a separate central review room, an unknown final approver, or an autonomous AI approval step. The concrete final reviewer is the owner in this dialogue.
