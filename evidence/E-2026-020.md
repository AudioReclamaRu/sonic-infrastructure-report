# E-2026-020

Short statement
The 2026 Infinite Dial (Edison Research at SSRS with the Audio Publishers Association,
10.06.2026): 58% of the U.S. 18+ population have ever listened to an audiobook — an
estimated 157 million people; among ever-listeners the top benefits are the ability
to multitask (86%), listening on-the-go (84%) and an alternative to screen time (70%).
