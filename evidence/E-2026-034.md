# E-2026-034

Short statement
On 30.10.2026 RadioToday reported that the WorldDAB Summit 2026 will be held next
month at Meliá Vienna in DC Tower 1, a building that also serves as a DAB+
transmission site for the Austrian capital, with sessions on DAB+ expansion,
radio regulation, in-car listening and the proposed EU Digital Networks Act, including
efforts to secure guaranteed FM and DAB+ receiver integration in vehicles. Around one
third of countries worldwide are now actively involved with DAB+, Poland has recently
invited applications for national multiplexes, and Germany has introduced permanent
Automatic Safety Alert broadcasts. Global sales of DAB and DAB+ receivers have exceeded
166 million units, according to figures confirmed at WorldDAB Automotive in June. The
opening debate "Why Radio Wins" features WorldDAB President Jacqueline Bierhorst,
Corinna Drumm of the Association of Austrian Private Broadcasters and Wouter Quartier,
Head of Radio at the European Broadcasting Union, moderated by UK DAB+ multiplex
operator Matt Deegan; Bierhorst will also give an international update covering trials,
launches and expansion in Ireland, Spain, Kenya and Thailand. Austria's market gets its
own focus: public broadcaster ORF pilots Hitradio Ö3 on DAB+, Digital Radio Austria
shares its promotion experience, and Markus Horvath of MediaMarkt explains the
retailer's decision to switch its in-store radio to digital broadcasting. WorldDAB
Project Director Bernie O'Neill said Vienna is "a dynamic DAB+ market with new and
trial services on air and strong promotion for the platform".
