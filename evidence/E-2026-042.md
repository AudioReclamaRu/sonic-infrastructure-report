# E-2026-042

## Short statement

В FAQ официального сайта Audio-Reclama.ru, прочитанном напрямую 15.11.2026, опубликованы: формат выдачи по умолчанию MP3 (16 bit, 44100 Hz, 320 kbps, stereo); бесплатная конвертация изготовленного файла в требуемый формат; примеры альтернативных форматов для обычной выдачи и телефонных систем; два режима дикторской записи — без обработки и с доведением до эфирного звучания; правило по дублям; отправка готового заказа по электронной почте.

**p = 1,0** относится к факту наличия именно этих формулировок на первичной странице в дату проверки. Это не доказательство внутреннего формата исходной записи, настроек DAW, параметров мастер-файла до кодирования или фактической конфигурации помещения.

## Directly observed statements

### 1. Default customer delivery

FAQ wording:

> «По умолчанию это mp3-файл (16 bit, 44100 Hz, 320 kbps, stereo).»

The page also states:

> «Любой, изготовленный у нас файл, конвертируется в нужный вам формат бесплатно.»

The FAQ says the finished order is sent by email.

**Interpretation boundary:** this is a published default delivery-file specification. It must not be re-labelled as the studio's raw capture setting, DAW session setting, archival master format or universal format for every deliverable. The FAQ literally combines an MP3 designation with a “16 bit” field; this record preserves the wording as published and does not reinterpret that field as a guaranteed PCM source bit depth.

### 2. Telephone / PBX compatibility

The FAQ explicitly warns that file parameters are needed for correct playback in the customer's PBX and says the necessary requirements should be requested from the PBX equipment supplier or IP-telephony operator.

An example on the same page is:

- file type: WAV;
- extension: PCM;
- sample rate: 8 kHz;
- bit depth: 8 bit;
- encoding: G.711 A-law.

This is a published example, not a universal PBX preset. Actual settings must be agreed with the customer's telephone platform.

### 3. Other requested delivery variants

The FAQ lists the following strings as common formats:

- wav, 44 khz, 16 бит, моно;
- wav, 8 khz, 8 бит, моно;
- mp3, 320 Кбит/с, моно;
- mp3, 192 Кбит/с, моно;
- 8K 8bit ULAW;
- PCM 11025 khz 8 бит, моно.

The last two examples and the written “khz” units are preserved literally. Their technical meaning and unit notation require confirmation before being normalized into a machine specification. The structured example above (WAV/PCM, 8 kHz, 8 bit, G.711 A-law) is clearer than those shorthand strings.

### 4. Dry recording and broadcast-finished voice

The FAQ distinguishes two services:

- **Recording without processing:** the customer receives the voice track as recorded; the page explicitly says there are no compressors or equalizers in this option.
- **Recording with broadcast finishing:** the page describes a processed voice track ready to be placed over music or video. It is described as an audio presentation without music or a radio spot without the musical arrangement.

The FAQ does not name the compressors, EQs, DAW, plugins, signal chain, target loudness or mastering standard. No such values are inferred here.

### 5. Takes / alternate performances

The FAQ says that, as a rule, a voice actor makes at least three takes for a commercial up to 45 seconds. For larger volumes, the page describes one main version according to the brief plus additional takes in passages where sound or pronunciation is debatable; the actor can reread passages after client feedback.

This is a published general process description, not a measured compliance audit of every historical order.

### 6. Music and rights context

The FAQ distinguishes original music written for a customer's specific order from licensed music selected or supplied from an external resource. It states that the customer receives full usage rights for the original music. This wording is recorded as a published FAQ statement; the exact contract and scope of rights for a particular order remain governed by that order's documents.
