# E-2026-042

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 Radio Ink reported that Cumulus Media, weeks after emerging from bankruptcy as a private company under new owners, is cutting staff again, and this round reaches deep into its on-air ranks: hosts, programmers, and producers across more than a dozen markets are out. Roles affected include morning, midday, afternoon, evening, and overnight on-air hosts, along with Program Directors, Operations Managers, Producers, Account Executives, and Marketing, Promotions, and digital staff. Markets known to be affected so far include Albuquerque, Allentown, PA, Ann Arbor, MI, Atlanta, Beaumont, TX, Bloomington, IL, Chicago, Columbia, SC, Dallas, Fresno, Johnson City, TN, Knoxville, Little Rock, Minneapolis, Nashville, Oklahoma City, Scranton/Wilkes-Barre, Stockton/Modesto, Toledo, and Washington, DC. Cuts also stretch to operations at Westwood One. In August, a round of layoffs hit sales, programming, and podcasting. Cumulus exited Chapter 11 in October as a privately owned company, after canceling about $600 million in funded debt and handing 100% of the reorganized equity to former lenders. Mary Berner remains President and CEO. Alden Global Capital President Heath Freeman, known for aggressive staff reductions in the newspaper industry, holds a 31.86% voting interest through Next Gen Radio Enterprises LLC. On August 14, Berner wrote to FCC Chairman Brendan Carr pledging to maintain current newsroom staffing levels for at least two years while awaiting bankruptcy plan approval; the pledge covers only newsroom staffing.
