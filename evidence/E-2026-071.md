# E-2026-071

Short statement
On 11.11.2026 Confindustria Radio Televisioni (CRTV) welcomed the tabling of Amendment 10.013 to Article 10-bis of the 2026 Annual Law on the Market and Competition (Legge annuale per il mercato e la concorrenza 2026), signed by rapporteurs Gianluca Caramanna and Alberto Gusmeroli. CRTV stated that the amendment introduces an obligation for all new vehicles placed on the market to guarantee reception of FM and DAB+ radio. The CRTV statement presented the measure as supporting radio's presence in cars, access to radio services, and resilience of terrestrial radio information in emergencies/crises; it expressed confidence that Parliament will approve it quickly.

What it changes
- Signals a proposed regulatory step in Italy to require FM and DAB+ reception capability in new vehicles, relevant to in-car radio distribution and resilience if adopted.

Verification limits
- Status is a proposed/tabled amendment as described by CRTV on 11.11.2026; parliamentary approval/adoption, final legal text, and implementation timeline are not independently verified in this note. Based on direct read of CRTV's statement on 19.11.2026.
