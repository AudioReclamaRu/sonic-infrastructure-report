# E-2026-028

Short statement
On 29.10.2026 Radio Ink reported Edison Research's third-quarter 2026 Share of Ear
data for in-car audio. Among Americans 13 and older whose primary vehicle is an
electric vehicle, AM/FM and its streams account for 42% of in-car audio time — the
largest share of any audio platform — against 53% for the total US population 13 and
older. Among EV drivers and riders streaming music is second at 22% (17% among all
listeners), SiriusXM rises from 16% to 20%, and the EV audience's share of in-car
time with podcasts is twice that of the total population. The EV figures are based on
160 respondents. Edison reports that the car accounts for over one-quarter of daily
audio listening time, an average of one hour and four minutes per day. Context in the
same article: the AM Radio for Every Vehicle Act waits in the Senate (H.R. 979 passed
the House by voice vote in September 2026; the Senate companion S. 315 has more than
60 bipartisan cosponsors); Q3 lobbying filings list Tesla ($650,000) and Lucid USA
($80,000) among opponents of the bill, about $6.3 million for the group in the
quarter; and a Critical Mass Insights study found that 96% of Americans said built-in
radio is important.
