# E-2026-063

Short statement
On 17.11.2026 RadioToday reported that Radio News Hub co-founders Stephanie Otty and Jamie Fletcher were named Creative and Media Entrepreneurs of the Year for the North East, Yorkshire and the Humber at the 2026 Allica Bank Great British Entrepreneur Awards in London on 16 November. They launched the Leeds-based news provider in 2015 to supply independent radio stations with national news services; it now serves more than 400 stations across the UK with hourly bulletins and a 20-minute Morning Report. The awards received 5,000 applications in 2026 with 740 businesses shortlisted.
