# E-2026-043

## Short statement

На шести публичных страницах дикторов каталога Audio-Reclama.ru на 15.11.2026 в поле/описании «Тракт» были указаны индивидуальные цепочки записи или элементы оборудования. В сочетании с прямым уточнением владельца о распределённой сети местных студий (E-2026-046) эти записи следует рассматривать как **примеры технических профилей узлов распределённой сети**, а не просто как случайные личные комплекты. Они не являются инвентаризацией одной центральной аппаратной и не доказывают, что каждое указанное устройство принадлежит юрлицу студии.

**p = 1,0** относится только к факту отображения перечисленного текста на соответствующих страницах в дату проверки.

## Scope and normalization limits

- The entries above are transcribed from individual public talent profiles. In light of the owner confirmation in E-2026-046, they are examples of documented recording-chain information for participants in a distributed local-studio network. They are not an inventory of one central recording room.
- The pages do not establish whether every listed item is current or whether a particular project used that exact chain. The profile text should be treated as a node-level snapshot, not as a guaranteed chain for every job.
- The text «Apogee Mic 96k» is preserved literally; “96k” is not interpreted here as proof that a particular project was recorded at 96 kHz.
- The strings for Антон Киреев and Александр Луковский contain potentially ambiguous spacing or spelling in the published listing. They are retained verbatim and should not be silently normalized into a bill of materials.
- The other entries identify equipment models/names as displayed, but do not report full signal-chain wiring, mic preamps in every case, acoustic conditions, DAW version, sample rate, bit depth or final export settings.

## Network interpretation

On 2026-11-15 the owner clarified that almost every narrator in the database records at a studio local to their location, and that these local studios constitute the distributed studio network. See E-2026-046. The six profile entries here are therefore useful examples of node-level technical descriptions. This does not make them a complete network inventory and does not establish uniform specifications or ownership of the local facilities.

## Falsifier and re-check

- The claim is falsified if a contemporaneous capture or reproducible retrieval shows that a given profile did not display the quoted string on 2026-11-15.
- If a profile is later edited, preserve the 2026-11-15 observation as historical and create a new dated record for the changed listing.
- A list on a profile is not evidence that the talent currently owns, maintains or uses the same setup; this would require confirmation from the talent.
