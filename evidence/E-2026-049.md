# E-2026-049

Short statement
On 17.11.2026 Radio Ink reported that MediaCo Holdings posted third-quarter 2026 revenue of $32.1 million, up 1.6% year over year (up 4.4% on a pro forma basis excluding the wound-down Estrella TV network). Digital revenue reached 31% of total revenue and grew 30.5% year over year to $9.9 million, including $4.8 million in audio (+34.4%) and $5.1 million in video (+27.0%). Spot and other revenue was $22.2 million, down 6.8%. Adjusted EBITDA was $5.8 million (18% margin), down from $7.8 million in the prior-year quarter. MediaCo reported a net loss of $30.5 million, compared with net income of $17.8 million a year earlier, reflecting a $30.2 million non-cash impairment charge tied to winding down Estrella TV network operations. For the first nine months of 2026, revenue was $95.5 million, up 3.4% year over year (up 6.8% pro forma excluding Estrella TV). CEO Albert Rodriguez said the company’s radio portfolio maintained steady, predictable performance while MediaCo is leaning further into digital and offering deeper, more integrated solutions across audio and video. The company’s radio brands include HOT 97 and WBLS in New York, and it owns the Don Cheto Radio Network.
