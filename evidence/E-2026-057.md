# E-2026-057

Short statement
On 17.11.2026 Rain News reported on a 27-page Sounds Profitable study of US podcast consumers: only 22% accept AI-generated hosts while 51% call them unacceptable, and 55% have seen or heard AI-made content. Some 30% of podcast consumers have used AI to discover shows and 60% of those found at least one show they stuck with; 18% felt tricked by unexpected AI audio. Weekly listeners in the AI-discovery group consume 6.7 hours a week against 5.5 hours for other listeners.
