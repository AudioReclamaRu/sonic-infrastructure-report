# E-2026-078

Short statement
On 12.11.2026 the UK Government confirmed that regulations had been made designating Amazon Alexa (including Alexa+), Google Assistant (including Gemini) and Apple Siri as Radio Selection Services (RSS) under Part 3B of the Communications Act 2003 (inserted by the Media Act 2024), as stated in the Government’s consultation outcome (updated 12.11.2026, direct read 20.11.2026). According to RadioToday (13.11.2026), designated platforms are expected, by early 2027, to be required to list and make available Ofcom-licensed UK radio stations on request or preset, must not override a user’s request for live radio and must follow the station’s own content/formatting unless permission is given, with protections for radio providers when changing metadata; RadioToday reported the measures are expected to affect around 700 million UK users.

What it changes
- Establishes a regulatory access/prominence regime for UK radio on major voice assistants/smart speakers: designated platforms face obligations on carriage, non-interference with live-radio requests, and respect for station content/metadata, shaping distribution discoverability and control in a key listening interface (framework per primary Government materials; detailed operational requirements/timing as reported).
