# E-2026-045

## Short statement

Несколько опубликованных кейсов Audio-Reclama.ru повторяют один и тот же набор производственных операций: кастинг дикторов, организация и ведение звукозаписывающего процесса, монтаж, пространственная, динамическая и частотная обработка, затем подготовка файлов под конкретную систему заказчика. Для видео в отдельных кейсах добавлены языковая проверка и сведение с изображением. Интервью со звукорежиссёром описывает внутреннее распределение заказов между звукорежиссёрами, его собственную запись дикторов и работу с установленным оборудованием и программным обеспечением студии.

**p = 1,0** относится к тому, что указанные сведения и формулировки присутствовали на первичных страницах Audio-Reclama.ru при прямом чтении 15.11.2026. Это не независимый аудит проектов, не технический лог каждой записи и не доказательство того, что абсолютно каждый заказ проходит все перечисленные стадии.

## Directly observed project details

### Repeated post-production sequence

The ABBYY, Vnukovo, RZD, Sberbank, Renaissance Credit and Moscow Business School case pages list a recurring set of steps, in the wording of the cases:

- casting of voice talents;
- organization and management of the recording process;
- editing of recordings;
- spatial, dynamic and frequency processing;
- production or preparation of deliverables for the target use/system (explicitly named for the telephone / announcement-system cases);
- transfer of usage rights in several cases.

The repeated wording supports a **published process pattern**. It does not disclose the plugin order, processor models, exact parameter values, loudness target, true-peak ceiling, sample rate, PCM bit depth or a formal QC checklist.

### Scale and deliverable examples

- **ABBYY:** the case says 214 telephone files were produced in MP3 at 256 kbps. It also lists casting, translation to English, organization of the recording process, editing, spatial/dynamic/frequency processing, finished auto-attendant production in the required format and licensed music.
- **Vnukovo:** the published case describes a full airport announcement-system update in 11 languages and lists casting, managed recording, editing, spatial/dynamic/frequency processing, preparation of files for the airport system and rights transfer.
- **Sberbank Volga-Vyatka:** the page states that the auto-informer comprised more than 1,500 files.
- **Renaissance Credit:** the page states that the auto-informer comprised more than 500 files.
- **RZD:** the page describes announcements for «Стриж» and «Сапсан» in Russian and English and lists the same core recording and post-production sequence, ending with preparation for the RZD system.
- **Braun CoolTec:** the case lists translation into six languages, proofreading and language monitoring, casting, managed recording, editing and spatial/dynamic/frequency processing, then synchronization/mixing with video.
- **Moscow Business School:** the page states that 48 audio summaries/books were produced and lists casting, managed recording, editing and spatial/dynamic/frequency processing.
- **Mercedes-Benz:** the page explicitly says that the recording process was organized at the BBC London studio, while editing and spatial/dynamic/frequency processing were part of the delivery work. This is evidence of a geographically separate recording location for this one project. It does not establish whether BBC London was a recurring network node, an external partner or a one-off arrangement.

These figures and descriptions are portfolio statements on the company's own website. They should be attributed to the company's case pages rather than represented as independently audited client records.

## What the sound-engineer interview adds

The interview page attributes the following first-person statements to Alexander Khonin:

- He describes a sound engineer's work as creating sound images, shaping dramaturgy and the overall sound concept, and recording, mixing and processing audio.
- He says he receives, processes and distributes incoming orders among sound engineers in the department, and also personally records voice talents and works on current orders.
- He refers to equipment installed in «our studio» and says he knows it in detail.
- He says software changes continuously, that the team studies new audio-processing plugins and software versions, and that each sound engineer has individual working tools / techniques.

The interview confirms that the studio publicly describes a technical base and a multi-engineer production department. It does **not** name the studio's microphone models, preamps, converters, monitors, DAWs or plugins. It also does not provide a room measurement, wiring diagram or versioned equipment inventory. The interview should be treated as first-person testimony published by the studio, not a dated equipment audit.

## What cannot be inferred

- The repeated sequence is not proof that every deliverable uses identical processing or that each named operation is always mandatory.
- “Spatial, dynamic and frequency processing” is not a complete signal chain and does not imply a specific reverb, compressor, EQ, denoiser or mastering suite.
- The stated MP3 256 kbps in the ABBYY case is a project-specific telephone deliverable; it is not the current general default and does not establish the capture format. Compare E-2026-042.
- More than 1,500 Sberbank files and more than 500 Renaissance Credit files are page claims; no underlying file manifest was published in these pages.
- The BBC London reference belongs to the Mercedes-Benz case only. It does not establish the node's contractual status, ownership or whether it belongs to the regular network.

## Falsifier and re-check

- The claim that each cited case page displayed the recorded wording on 2026-11-15 is falsified by a contemporaneous capture or reproducible retrieval showing otherwise.
- A page update after the observation date should be recorded as a new dated observation, preserving this historical record.
- Project totals should not be presented as independently verified client metrics unless supporting manifests or client records become available.
- Statements in the interview should not be converted into a current equipment list without a dated confirmation from the studio.
