# E-2026-045

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 Voice Over Herald reported that Voicemod advertised a free "Cyn voice changer from Murder Drones" and that Allanah "Fitzy" Fitzgerald, the Australian actor who voices Cyn, said nobody had asked her. The post went up on Voicemod's X account at 7 p.m. UTC on Saturday, November 14, 2026, with a short vertical video and the line "FREE Cyn voice changer from Murder Drones", followed by the code VOICEMODDRYN; the video shows a new voice called Dryn appearing in the app, its avatar wearing a blonde fringe, a maid's cap and two X-shaped eyes. Fitzgerald answered early on Sunday, writing on X that the company "has taken my voice and is selling it to people who use their platform", and adding: "I did not approve this, I was not even ASKED." Cyn is the system administrator at the center of Murder Drones, the eight-episode animated series Glitch Productions released on YouTube between October 2021 and August 2024; Fitzgerald also voices the Absolute Solver. Inside the app and on Voicemod's voice library site the voice is called Dryn, listed as an "Official AI Voice", "Crafted by Voicemod"; that page does not mention Cyn, Murder Drones, Glitch Productions or Fitzgerald, and does not say whose recordings the voice was built from. Voice Over Herald found no public reply from Voicemod by Sunday afternoon, New York time, and its promotional post was still up.
