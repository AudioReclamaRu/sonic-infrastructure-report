# E-2026-067

Short statement
On 03.11.2026 the Government of Spain (Ministerio para la Transformación Digital y de la Función Pública, Secretaría de Estado de Telecomunicaciones e Infraestructuras Digitales) announced approval by the Council of Ministers of a Real Decreto to implement digital terrestrial radio with DAB+ technology in Spain. The official note states that migration from DAB to DAB+ can be done by adapting signal encoding without replacing transmission infrastructure or switching off DAB; DAB+ makes audio more robust against interference and enables advanced audio formats and Automatic Safety Alert (ASA).

The legal text, Real Decreto 958/2026, de 4 de noviembre (BOE núm. 276, 05.11.2026, BOE-A-2026-23177), confirms and details the framework (direct read of BOE text 19.11.2026): it enters into force three months after the day following publication (06.02.2027). State-level digital terrestrial sound broadcasting must be provided exclusively with DAB+ (art. 3.1). For autonomous and local services, emissions are initially provided with DAB, but each holder may voluntarily and unilaterally provide the service with DAB+ after prior communication to the competent audiovisual body of the autonomous community (without exceeding licence conditions), and the competent body may decide the date from which emissions in its networks must be exclusively DAB+ (art. 4.2–4.3). Networks emitting exclusively with DAB+ may integrate up to 12 channels (vs. 6 for DAB) (art. 2.4). Networks emitting exclusively and obligatorily with DAB+ must be capable of managing and sending public alerts via the Automatic Safety Alert system (ASA) (art. 6).

For RTVE’s state public service network (MF-I), Disposición adicional primera sets phased coverage obligations: at least 50% of the population within 6 months of entry into force; at least 70% within 18 months (including at least 50% of the population of each provincial capital, and 50% of motorways and dual carriageways, autopistas y autovías, in that period); and at least 80% within 30 months (including at least 70% of towns with more than 25,000 inhabitants, and 70% of motorways and dual carriageways in that period).

Verification limits
- Core policy, timing (publication/entry into force), state-level DAB+ exclusivity, voluntary/competence-based transition for autonomous/local services, channel-capacity and ASA provisions, and RTVE phased coverage targets/thresholds are verified from the BOE legal text (direct read 19.11.2026) and the official press note (direct read 19.11.2026).
- The BOE preamble states the impulse measures do not establish DAB “switch-off” phases and that the DAB→DAB+ improvement is achieved by a change in audio-signal encoding without impact on the existing transmission network. Practical rollout schedules beyond the legal targets above, reception-device market effects, and implementation details not stated in the note/BOE text are not asserted.
