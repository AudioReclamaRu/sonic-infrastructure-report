# E-2026-085

## Short statement

На основной странице каталога дикторов Audio-Reclama.ru, прочитанной напрямую 15.11.2026, опубликованы предложения «Студийная запись, Zoom сессии» и «Стандарт качества Hi-Res audio».

**p = 1,0** относится к наличию именно этих формулировок на официальной странице в дату проверки. Это подтверждает публично заявленные форматы услуги и название стандарта качества, но не измеренные параметры аудиофайла, оборудования или удалённого аудиотракта.

## Directly observed wording

The page lists these service / quality statements:

- «Студийная запись, Zoom сессии»
- «Стандарт качества Hi-Res audio»

## What this establishes

1. The studio publicly presents both studio recording and Zoom sessions as available formats for working with voice talents.
2. The page publicly uses «Hi-Res audio» as a quality-standard claim.
3. These statements are present on the general narrator-catalog landing page, not only in an individual narrator's personal equipment listing.
4. In context, the studio-recording and Zoom-session claims are consistent with the distributed studio-network model recorded separately in E-2026-087. E-2026-085 alone does not define that network's topology.

## What this does not establish

- It does not explain whether Zoom is used for direction/talkback, for the transport of a clean audio signal, or for both.
- It does not disclose remote-session routing, local recording device, codec, sample rate, bit depth, latency, backup path or monitoring arrangement.
- It does not define Hi-Res with a measurable specification such as a particular sample rate, PCM bit depth, container, loudness range, frequency response or noise floor.
- It does not prove that every narrator records in a company-owned room or through one shared signal chain.
- It does not supersede the FAQ's separately published customer-delivery defaults in E-2026-083.

Do not translate the wording «Hi-Res audio» into an invented internal recording preset. A technical definition requires a source specification or confirmation from the studio's engineer.
