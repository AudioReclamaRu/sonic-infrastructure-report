## What can and cannot be concluded

**Observed:** Metricool currently records one TikTok post in the requested 30-day window and zero recorded views/interactions for that post.

**Not established:**
- that TikTok has shadow-banned or suppressed the account;
- that the video is ineligible for For You;
- that the account's true lifetime follower count is zero;
- that the video was never served to anyone (Metricool was linked only on the observation date);
- that the creative itself failed, because there are no viewer or retention observations to evaluate.

The immediate problem is therefore a distribution/eligibility/data-verification gap, not yet a demonstrated creative-retention problem.

## Required in-app verification

TikTok's official help says a post can be made ineligible for For You; the post owner can read the reason in TikTok Studio analytics and appeal. It also says content under review may be removed or made ineligible.

1. Open the published post in the TikTok app and confirm privacy is **Everyone/Public**, not Friends or Only you.
2. Open **More insights → Analytics** for the post. Check for a notification that the video is ineligible for the For You feed; inspect the stated reason and use **Appeal** if the decision is incorrect.
3. Check **Inbox → System notifications → Account updates** for moderation or account restrictions; use Account check to inspect any strikes.
4. Do not delete and re-upload the same file as a workaround. Preserve the original post and its state while collecting evidence.

Official sources:
- https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/content-violations-and-bans
- https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content

TikTok states that recommendation can use user interactions (including full watches and skips), content information (including sounds and hashtags), and user information; for most users, interactions such as time spent watching are generally weighted more heavily than other factors. This is why retention and distribution-source data must be collected before judging the creative.

## Three-post TikTok test — production brief

Run only after the public-visibility and For You eligibility checks above. Keep each clip original and meaningfully different; do not repost the existing file.

The studio's pre-language creative rule applies inside each video: no spoken narration, dialogue, lyrics, on-screen text, subtitles or captions. Convey the idea through visual action, sound design, rhythm, light, silence and music without words. The post caption outside the video may be Russian text.

### Test A — «Сырой звук → готовый звук»

- **Length:** 12–15 s.
- **First second:** immediate audible and visual contrast; begin with a deliberately dry/noisy room tone and a sharply visible recording setup.
- **Middle:** show one clear change in environment/recording chain; sound transitions from untreated to controlled studio sound.
- **End:** return to the opening frame or sound for a seamless loop.
- **Caption hypothesis:** explain in one sentence why the same voice can sound different in a room and in a prepared recording environment.
- **Primary measures:** For You share, average watch time, full-watch rate, shares.

### Test B — «Голосовое меню: путь или лабиринт»

- **Length:** 12–15 s.
- **First second:** a visual sequence branches into confusing paths, then resolves into one simple illuminated route.
- **Sound:** a restrained non-verbal pulse; no spoken menu, digits or text.
- **Caption hypothesis:** a business IVR should help a caller reach the needed destination without getting trapped in a menu.
- **Primary measures:** For You share, average watch time, comments, profile visits.

### Test C — «Один объект, один точный звук»

- **Length:** 12–15 s.
- **First second:** a familiar object appears visually ordinary; its sound creates the reveal.
- **Middle:** show three different sound-design treatments of the same action, then select one coherent final sound.
- **Caption hypothesis:** a sound detail can make a brand, video or message feel intentional; it is not merely background music.
- **Primary measures:** average watch time, full-watch rate, shares and profile visits.

## Measurement protocol

Capture each post at approximately 2 h, 24 h and 72 h after publication:
- views and reach;
- For You / Following / Search / Hashtag / Sound / Profile source shares, when available;
- average watch time and full-video watched rate;
- likes, comments, saves and shares;
- profile visits and new followers, if the connector exposes them.

Interpretation:
- **0 views + ineligible notice:** resolve eligibility/review first; do not change creative as a substitute for an appeal.
- **0 views + no restriction notice:** verify public privacy and public accessibility; then check that the post exists on the public profile and compare TikTok Studio's own analytics with Metricool.
- **Views > 0 but low watch/finish:** creative hook, pacing or payoff is the next variable to change.
- **For You traffic > 0:** recommendation has begun; optimise retention and sharing rather than changing upload time based on guesses.

Three posts are an initial diagnostic series, not a statistically conclusive experiment. Do not claim a growth rate or successful channel strategy from this sample.

## Guardrails

- No paid promotion, purchased views, engagement pods, fake accounts, region spoofing or paid analytics credits.
- Do not claim shadowban without TikTok's own account/post status evidence.
- Do not make website or profile changes in this audit.
