# E-2026-070

Short statement
On 17.11.2026 MDR stated that MDR SPUTNIK will switch entirely to digital distribution paths from 2027. FM (UKW) transmission will be discontinued by February 2027 at the latest. The programme will remain available via DAB+, ARD Sounds, livestream and sputnik.de. MDR framed the move as a response to changed media use and as part of its digital transition, noting UKW as the last remaining analogue distribution path for MDR radio programmes.

What it changes
- Sets a concrete timeline for MDR SPUTNIK to exit FM and become digital-only (by February 2027 at the latest), shifting a public youth radio service in Germany fully to DAB+ and online distribution.

Verification limits
- Based on direct read of the MDR SPUTNIK FAQ page on 19.11.2026. Audience, technical coverage details and any effects beyond what MDR states are not independently verified in this note.
