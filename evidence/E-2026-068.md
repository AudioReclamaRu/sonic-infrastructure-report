## Interpretation and limits

1. Radio advertising has the clearest near-page-one demand signal: several buyer-intent phrases already appear around positions 4–8, yet the page gets almost no clicks for those exact phrases. This merits a snippet/message and SERP-comparison review; the data alone does not establish why the result is not clicked.
2. IVR, video voiceover, and audiobook queries show relevant demand but many terms rank beyond position 15–20. They need a relevance/coverage and internal-linking review before any snippet-only intervention.
3. The audiobook page does receive clicks, but its average position is 27.8; its click-driving query rows are mainly price/quotation questions. A clearer pricing explanation and scope/estimate expectations are hypotheses to test, not verified causes.
4. Search Console measures impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position. It does not show enquiries, qualified leads, calls, or revenue.
5. No GA4 property is currently bound to the OpenSEO project; the GA4 property-list check returned no visible properties. Windsor.ai's connected GA4 account returned no rows in the last-30-day test. This is a measurement/integration gap, not proof that the site had no visits.
6. Results are property- and search-type-specific; low-volume query rows may be suppressed or incomplete. Do not sum page and query views as if they were independent totals.

## Safe next checks

- Compare title/snippet and competing SERP results for the radio page; check that the result communicates service, sample audio, turnaround, deliverables, and next step accurately.
- Review the IVR and video-voiceover page content against the exact query families above; prioritize query relevance, visible proof/examples, process, technical deliverables, and a clear quote path.
- For audiobooks, create a transparent estimator/brief checklist as a draft for owner review; do not invent per-minute prices or delivery promises.
- Connect the correct GA4 property to OpenSEO and verify key events (form submission, telephone click, mailto, and messaging links) before claiming conversion performance.
- Keep the official website read-only until separately authorized.
