# E-2026-068

Short statement
On 13.11.2026 Český rozhlas (digital.rozhlas.cz) reported that from 12.11.2026, 14:00, the stations RockZone and Český Impuls gained the option of regional switching in the nationwide DAB+ multiplex B operated by České Radiokomunikace (CRA). The two stations, part of the Londa media group, can now broadcast separately for Bohemia and separately for Moravia, allowing different content in each part (for example regional advertising, news or trailers).

Key details (as stated by the source)
- Both stations have been broadcast in CRA’s network since 23.04 last year together with Rádio Impuls, each with a 48 kbps stereo stream. In analogue broadcasting they were only regional or local (RockZone on FM/VKV in Prague; Český Impuls on medium wave/AM).
- The nationwide CRA network is divided into eight regional areas enabling different content by area; Londa is initially using a coarser split into two mutations (Bohemia and Moravia).
- For advertisers this enables targeting advertising to only one part of the country rather than buying nationwide reach.
- Listeners usually do not need to retune; receivers pick up the mutation in their region, but near the Bohemia–Moravia boundary a receiver may show two versions or switch to the stronger signal.

What it changes
- Activates regionalized distribution and ad/news insertion capability for commercial services carried in a nationwide Czech DAB+ multiplex (technical/monetization flexibility within DAB+ carriage), rather than only uniform nationwide output.

Verification limits
- Based on direct read of the Český rozhlas digital.rozhlas.cz report on 19.11.2026. Commercial terms, actual regional ad campaigns, and any effects beyond what the source states are not independently verified in this note.
