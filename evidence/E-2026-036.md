# E-2026-036

Short statement
On 04.11.2026 Radio Ink reported on the MIW webinar "AI for Content Creators", where four
US radio air talents described how they use generative tools in daily show prep, spots and
social clips. Panellists: Meg Dowdy (Townsquare Media regional content director, national
contesting director, MIW board member), Andre Yancey (brand manager, Audacy 102 JAMS/KRBQ
San Francisco), Lauren "Lo" Sessions (host, 93Q/KKBQ Houston) and Carolyn McArdle and Cort
Johnson (co-hosts, iHeartMedia San Francisco's The Morning Breeze). Sessions calls AI a
brainstorming partner and says "AI doesn't know what it's like to be me"; she turns a 60 to
90 second on-air pop-culture feature into social video because "we don't always need to
create more content" but should get more mileage from it. Yancey built a prompt around the
style of a personality he had worked with, Ed Lover, and warns that "the fact-checking isn't
there because we know AI hallucinates more than it doesn't"; he also runs air checks through
Opus and CapCut to cut shorter clips. McArdle and Johnson use AI to draft endorsement and
spec spots from client copy points: McArdle says it saves "a ton of time" but the tool
"leaves details out that are tremendously important, like a phone number or a website", so
she rewrites the copy; Johnson pulls the draft into a Word document and rewrites it "using
the bones that AI gave me". Dowdy described a three-step "meta-prompting" method: give the
AI a role, context and a clear goal, then ask what it needs to write the prompt. The
cautionary tale came from McArdle: after asking a chatbot to rewrite an endorsement with the
instruction "write again, but change it up", the model inserted "would you like your
endorsement to blank, blank, blank?" into the script, and she was already reading the spot
live on air; she dropped out of the copy and finished with a line pointing listeners to the
client. MIW plans a full schedule of free educational webinars for 2027.
