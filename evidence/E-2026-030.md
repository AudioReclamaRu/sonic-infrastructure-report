# E-2026-030

Short statement
On 21.10.2026 Radio Ink published the closing webinar of The Podcast Study 2026
by Strategic Solutions Research and Point-To-Point Marketing, based on 1,205
podcast consumers aged 18 to 54: 74% listen to local radio weekly or more and
46% listen every day. Among radio listeners with a favorite podcast, 81% say the
host is a big part of, or the only reason for, their listening; among daily radio
listeners 24% name the host as the only reason and 52% are likely to tune in for
a chance to interact with the host. 55% of radio listeners give a new podcast
five minutes or less before deciding, and 60% of daily radio listeners decide
within five minutes. 47% of daily radio listeners are strongly influenced by
host-read ads; after a host endorsement radio listeners are more likely to notice
the ad (54%), remember the brand (56%), trust the ad (49%) and consider buying
(46%). 54% of radio listeners say podcasts carry too many ads, 46% say there are
more than a year ago, and 21% listen to podcasts less because of ads. The 26% who
listen to local radio less than weekly are mostly lapsed: 75% have listened
before and 48% still listen at least every few months; 59% of lapsed listeners
skip ads versus 48% of radio listeners, and 41% skip even when the host is one
they like versus 24%. 65% of radio listeners typically see two or more clips
before trying a new podcast, 43% are moved by recognizing or liking the host and
43% by a single clip that made them feel they were missing out. Superfans make up
17% of the sample and Prime Prospects 18%; 79% of Superfans listen to local radio
weekly or more and 58% daily, while 82% of Prime Prospects listen weekly or more
and 56% daily. Among daily radio listeners 55% would definitely join a free VIP
club and 44% would definitely open an email from the host; among radio-listening
Prime Prospects 75% are likely to subscribe to a host-read-only tier.
