# E-2026-016

Short statement
On 10.09.2026 Universal Music Group (world's largest music company) and
ElevenLabs announced a multi-year licensing agreement and wider strategic
collaboration — the first between a major label and an AI-audio company —
beginning with a licensed AI music creation platform (remixes, mashups, new
interpretations, personalized vocal experiences). The deal is framed around
rights and value-sharing: licensed music, artist participation, fair
compensation, "respect human artistry". Quotes: Sir Lucian Grainge (Chairman
& CEO, UMG), Mati Staniszewski (cofounder & CEO, ElevenLabs). ElevenLabs
scope note: AI voice/audio across 70+ languages; employees at two-thirds of
Fortune 500 use its products.
