# E-2026-064

Short statement
On 17.11.2026 RadioToday reported that SRG SSR restored FM transmissions on more than 30 transmitter sites on Thursday 12 November, returning FM coverage to approximately 80% of the Swiss population. The network had been closed at the end of 2024, but in December 2025 Parliament decided to allow analogue broadcasting beyond 2026. SRG estimates the extra cost of the restored network at about 6.5 million Swiss francs a year against roughly 15 million francs for its former full-coverage FM service.
