# E-2026-066

Short statement
On 19.11.2026 RadioToday reported that Arqiva expanded Sound Digital's national DAB multiplex with 32 new transmitters, bringing coverage to more than 1.1 million additional households and improving reception across more than 950 miles of major roads in England, Scotland and Wales. Of the 32 sites, 18 were already on air, with the remaining transmitters scheduled to go live before Christmas and the full project due for completion by Easter 2027. Sound Digital, a joint venture between Arqiva, Bauer Media Audio UK and News UK, carries 30 national services.
