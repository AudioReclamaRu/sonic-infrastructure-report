# E-2026-074

Short statement
Budget 2026 (Government of Canada, tabled 4 November 2026) proposes to extend the Canadian Journalism Labour Tax Credit (CJLTC) to qualifying private audio and audiovisual news organisations, in line with support provided to news organisations that produce written news. Under the proposal, they would be eligible for the same temporarily enhanced credit rate of 35% and annual labour expenditure limit of $85,000 per employee, with changes effective for labour expenses incurred on or after Budget Day. Chapter 4 also notes that the Spring Economic Update 2026 announced the government's intent to extend eligibility to audio and audiovisual news production and that consultations informed Budget 2026.

What it changes
- Extends a federal journalism labour tax credit framework to qualifying private audio and audiovisual news organisations (including radio/audio news contexts), aligning support with written-news organisations and affecting financial conditions for private audio news in Canada.
