# E-2026-052

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 TechCrunch reported that Audible launched an audio discovery feature that plays a short AI-generated overview of a book themes and a summary of what readers liked, together with commentary from an Audible editor or author, next to the narrated sample. The overview is generated with AI, while the commentary is drawn from recordings of past Audible interviews and publicly available reviews. The feature starts with select English-language titles in the US; Recaps and the interactive Ask a Question tool expand to more US titles and become broadly available in December. The report notes that Spotify audiobook listening expanded to more than 180 markets the previous month.
