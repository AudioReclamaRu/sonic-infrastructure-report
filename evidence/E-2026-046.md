# E-2026-046

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 TV Technology reported that Apple TV is adding a video podcasts section to its lineup in a beta test with developers, according to the New York Times, and that it is anticipated Apple will make it free to anyone with the Apple TV app, available on Rokus and other smart TVs. The article, by Tom Butts, said Apple debuted video podcasts on its Podcast app earlier this year and is expected to increase that number in coming months. TV Technology noted that video podcasts have become among the most popular programs on YouTube and Spotify, and that Apple TV competitors Hulu and Netflix have also enhanced their video podcast strategies. The report added that iHeartMedia is enhancing its podcast offerings by adding full-length video podcast distribution to iHeartRadio, a move the company said is designed to expand reach for creators. The article also noted that the name "podcast", coined in 2004, was literally a variation on Apple's iPod and "broadcast".
