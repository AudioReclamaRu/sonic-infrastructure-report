# E-2026-046

## Short statement

Владелец «Аудио-Реклама•ru» прямо уточнил 15.11.2026, что **почти каждый диктор в базе производит запись на студии по месту своего расположения; именно это и является распределённой сетью студий**.

**p = 1,0** относится ровно к этой операционной характеристике, подтверждённой владельцем в рабочем диалоге. Это первичное внутреннее подтверждение, а не независимая внешняя проверка полного состава сети.

## Primary source and exact owner statement

- Source: first-party owner clarification in the project conversation.
- Date: 2026-11-15.
- Exact wording: «почти каждый диктор в нашей базе производит запись на студии по месту своего расположения - это и есть распределенная сеть студий».
- This record does not reproduce or expose private contact details or unrelated personal information.

## Operational meaning

The operating model should be understood as a **distributed network of local recording studios**, not merely as a single central recording room with a directory of independent home-recording talents.

- For almost every narrator in the database, recording is performed at a studio local to the narrator's location.
- The technical recording points are geographically distributed.
- Public talent-profile entries that identify a recording chain are therefore potentially visible descriptions of a network node's technical setup, rather than unrelated personal gear lists. See E-2026-043.
- The network may contain heterogeneous equipment and rooms; no uniform hardware or acoustic specification is asserted.
- Public case pages and the sound-engineer interview document coordinated production and post-production steps, but do not by themselves specify exactly which stages occur at each local node or at a central physical site. See E-2026-045.
- The public phrase «Zoom сессии» is compatible with a distributed session model, but does not identify the exact role of Zoom in direction, talkback, monitoring or the clean recording signal path. See E-2026-044.

## What this does not establish

This owner clarification does not, by itself, establish:

- an exact count or percentage of active studios/nodes;
- a complete or current city/country map of nodes;
- ownership of each local studio or whether every node is an exclusive contractual partner;
- certification, uniform inspection or equal acoustic performance across all locations;
- current accuracy of every model listed on a narrator profile;
- a single shared microphone/preamp/interface/DAW chain;
- a unified recording format, loudness target, noise-floor limit, RT60 target or formal node acceptance checklist;
- exactly where editing, mixing, QC and final mastering are performed for every project.

The phrase «почти каждый» is qualitative and must remain so until the network roster is enumerated and dated. Do not convert it into a percentage.

## Falsifier and re-check

- The statement would need correction if the owner revises the operating description or a dated, complete network roster demonstrates that local-studio recording is not the normal arrangement for almost all listed narrators.
- A future count or node inventory must be added as a separate dated observation, not retroactively inferred from this qualitative statement.
- The six public profile chains in E-2026-043 are examples, not a complete list of the network.
