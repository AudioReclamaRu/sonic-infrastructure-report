# E-2026-047

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 RadioToday reported that only 34% of podcast brand and show pairings from the first quarter of 2026 reappeared in the second quarter. The finding comes from a study of 125,429 pairings by Podmerse, a new database for podcast publishers and media buyers. The analysis excluded podcasts that stopped running advertising in the second quarter, and it found that 66% of the brands recorded on a particular show in the first quarter were not recorded on that same show in the following three months. Podmerse launched publicly on 28 October and says it has records covering more than two million sponsored episodes across nearly 44,000 podcasts and more than 368,000 brands. The service analyses podcast episodes to identify presenter reads and dynamically inserted advertising, recording the sponsoring brand, programme, number of episodes sponsored and date of its most recent appearance; users can search by industry and category, save lists and export information, and the database is updated daily. Co-founder Anuj Agarwal said naming the twenty brands buying ads on three shows is a week of listening, while fellow co-founder Vineet Agarwal said the useful question is which brands came back to a show a second and a third time. The service has free and paid plans, though the company acknowledges automated detection can occasionally produce false positives.
