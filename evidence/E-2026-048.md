# E-2026-048

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 RadioToday reported that The Vinyl Countdown has signed up its 100th radio station, eight years after beginning as a podcast for a presenter's young son. Former BBC News television foreign correspondent and presenter Jonathan Charles started the programme in 2018 to share his interest in vinyl records with his then ten year old son, wanting to discover what a younger listener would make of singles from the 1960s, 1970s and 1980s released long before his son was born. Radio stations subsequently approached Jonathan about broadcasting the programme, which has developed into a syndicated weekly hour long show, and he selects the music each week from his own record collection. Stations that have signed up include KL1 Radio in the East of England, Marlow FM, Durham OnAir and The Cat 107.9 FM in Crewe; Marlow FM broadcasts it every weekday morning at 9am while Durham OnAir airs it on Thursday nights. Jonathan said he could not believe that what started as a project to educate his young son about the music he loves has turned into such a successful radio show, and, after a career reporting on wars and other serious events, added that he is glad he got the chance to go from being a news junkie to a DJ.
