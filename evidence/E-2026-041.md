# E-2026-041

Short statement
On 09.11.2026 RadioInsight reported that iHeartMedia released its earnings report for the third quarter ending on September 30. Q3 revenue of $1.03 billion was up 3.3% over the same quarter in 2025 and, excluding political revenue, up 0.9%. GAAP operating income was $73 million, compared to a GAAP operating loss of $116 million in Q3 2025. Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA was $170 million, compared to $205 million in Q3 2025. The Multiplatform Group, which includes radio station operations, saw revenue of $562 million, down 5% from last year; excluding political revenue it was down 7%. That segment's Adjusted EBITDA was $68 million, down 43%, and its Adjusted EBITDA margin was 12.1%. The Audio & Media Services Group, which includes Katz and RCS, saw revenue up 23% to $82 million, which dropped to 3% excluding political revenue. The Digital Audio Group, which includes podcast and streaming, was up 14% to $389 million. Podcast revenue itself was up 24% to $174 million, with other digital revenue up 6% to $215 million. Total programmatic revenue was $200 million, up 50% from last year, and the company expects it to double to $400 million in 2027, including $100 million of incremental broadcast radio programmatic revenue. Chairman/CEO Bob Pittman said consolidated revenue was $1.03 billion, up 3.3%, and ex political up 0.9%, with Adjusted EBITDA of $170 million. President/COO Rich Bressler said the Digital Audio Group's revenue was $389 million, up 13.8%, with Q3 Adjusted EBITDA margins of 32.8%, and that from 2027 the company expects to establish podcasting as a separate reporting segment. Yahoo Finance independently reported the same $1.03 billion revenue and 3.3% growth while noting that the stock plunged 36.9% to $1.12 after the results, that revenue missed the $1.05 billion consensus by 2.2%, and that GAAP EPS was negative $0.06.
