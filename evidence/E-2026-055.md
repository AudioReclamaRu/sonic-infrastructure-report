# E-2026-055

Short statement
On 16.11.2026 Radio Ink reported that Beasley Media Group third-quarter net revenue fell 16.1% to $42.8 million from $51.0 million, or 10.2% on a same-station basis. The net loss widened to $12.1 million from $3.6 million, largely because of a $13.1 million write-down of FCC licenses. Digital revenue was $9.8 million, down 24% overall, and made up 23% of net revenue. Long-term debt fell to $32.0 million from $235.3 million, while working capital moved to a $95.1 million deficit.
