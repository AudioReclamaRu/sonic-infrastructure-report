# Universal Music Group

Canonical ID: ENTITY-UMG

Definition
The world's largest music-based entertainment company. On 10.09.2026 signed
its first AI-audio licensing agreement and strategic collaboration with
ElevenLabs — the first between a major label and an AI-audio company —
beginning with a licensed AI music creation platform (remixes, mashups, new
interpretations, personalized vocal experiences). Deal is framed around
rights and value-sharing, not substitution.
