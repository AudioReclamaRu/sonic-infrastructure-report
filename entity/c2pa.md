# C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity)

Canonical ID: ENTITY-C2PA

Definition
Open technical standard for content provenance (Content Credentials): signed
manifests bind a media asset to its origin. The reference layer the
repository's verification chain maps onto for machine-readable, cross-platform
provenance of voice assets.
