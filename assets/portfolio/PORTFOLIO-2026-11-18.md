## Portfolio cases — structured index

### 1. Vnukovo International Airport

The portfolio states that the studio completely re-voiced the airport's voice system in 11 languages: Russian, English, German, Spanish, Greek, Turkish, Azerbaijani, Moldavian, Uzbek, “Tadjik” (spelled this way in the source), and Kazakh.

Work listed:
- voice-over casting;
- organization and management of the sound-recording process;
- audio mastering, including spatial-audio mastering, dynamic-range compression and equalization;
- file post-production for the airport download system;
- legal support covering copyrights and workflow.

Location shown in portfolio: Moscow, Russia.
