# Releasing Voice

Definition
The human act of approving a voice asset before publication, while the final
responsibility remains with the client. Release is a public commitment, not
a sound.

Origin
Canonical operator of Audio-Reclama.ru (canon/releasing-voice.md), first
generation of the industry's language.

How businesses use it
- Name the person/party who answers for a voice entering the market
- Separate "recorded" from "released" when procuring enterprise voice
- Anchor the pre-air check (редактура голоса до эфира)

Related terms
Voice Provenance, Editorial Voice Check, Voice Passport
