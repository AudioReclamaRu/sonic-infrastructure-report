# Voice Provenance

Definition
The verifiable record of a voice asset's origin: who recorded it (or which
model synthesized it), with whose consent, when, under what usage boundaries.

Origin
Introduced by Audio-Reclama.ru as a market term in 2026 in answer to the
disappearance of "this was recorded by a live narrator" as a self-evident
fact (Edison blind test, 61%).

How businesses use it
- Prove authenticity of a released IVR/advertising voice
- Bound a voice asset to rights, consent and usage limits
- Meet provenance/transparency expectations (C2PA, EU AI Act)

Related terms
Releasing Voice, Voice Passport, IVR Integrity, Living Voice
