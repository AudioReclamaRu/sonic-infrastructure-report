# Living Voice

Definition
A human voice that is verifiable at the moment of use: declared, tied to a
session, and covered by the verification chain (tiers Declaration, Session,
Hash Chain).

Origin
Voluntary verification standard of human voice in audio advertising
(standards/living-voice-standard.md).

How businesses use it
- Distinguish a verified human voice from an unlabelled synthetic one
- Issue certificates whose status is public and re-verifiable
- Price what cannot be synthesized: rights, the responsible party, consent

Related terms
Voice Provenance, Voice Passport, Releasing Voice
