# Field of Belonging

Definition
An environment creates return before attention becomes a goal. The field
leaves a place and closes nothing; the entrance is always on the guest's side.

Origin
Canonical operator of Audio-Reclama.ru (canon/field-of-belonging.md),
поле принадлежности.

How businesses use it
- Replace attention capture with a place people return to on their own
- Design IVR, podcasts and corporate voices as environments, not messages
- Measure belonging by return, not by audience size

Related terms
Law of Ritual, Right to Be Present, Releasing Voice
