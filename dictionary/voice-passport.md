# Voice Passport

Definition
A document that identifies a recording: phonogram ID, date, voice (narrator
or model), consent, rights volume, usage boundaries, and the party answering
for the release.

Origin
Audio-Reclama.ru provenance standard
(standards/voice-provenance-passport.md).

How businesses use it
- Re-attach a recording to a figure, date and responsibility when voice is no
  longer self-evident
- Attach the final version digest and rights bounds to each release
- Produce during pre-air voice editing (редактура голоса до эфира)

Related terms
Voice Provenance, Releasing Voice, IVR Integrity
