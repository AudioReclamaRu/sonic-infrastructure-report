# Public Media Content Audit — 2026-11-17

## Purpose and evidence rules

This is a read-only snapshot of publicly discoverable media for **Аудио-Реклама•ru**. It is not a channel analytics export and does not prove account ownership, administrator access, audience geography, rights clearance, or current commercial availability.

Evidence classes used here:

- **Direct public page/search-index evidence:** a public platform result exposes the title, creator attribution, description, date, or displayed metric.
- **Previously recorded snapshot:** an earlier research record exists, but its metrics were not re-collected in this pass.
- **User-identified URL:** the user supplied the URL; page identity and access remain unverified.
- **Unavailable:** the page could not be inspected in this pass. This is not evidence of absence.

Metrics are volatile and must always be stored with the capture date and source. Search snippets may be stale or inconsistent. Do not treat search-index snippets as current channel analytics.

## Executive findings

1. **RUTUBE has a discoverable portfolio footprint**, including service explainers, voice-over/dubbing examples, advertising videos, voice demos and short clips. The sampled public cards show very low displayed engagement: typically 1–5 views, with creator follower/subscriber labels varying between 1 and 3 across sampled cards. These are page-level snapshots, not channel analytics.
2. **The content mix is broad but fragmented.** Titles range from descriptive service labels (e.g. video voice-over, advertising video, IVR/voice greeting) to generic clip labels ("Clip 1", "Clip 2", "Clip 4") and brand slogans. Generic titles are weak for search discovery because they do not identify the service, client problem, language, voice talent or use case.
3. **A stale-price risk is present.** One RUTUBE video description publicly states 58,000 ₽ for a 30-second commercial, 55,000 ₽ for an IVR greeting, and 72 hours from payment. This is an observed historical statement only; current validity is unknown. Do not copy it into current price pages, AI answer prompts, profile descriptions or sales materials until checked against an approved current tariff.
4. **Date and metadata inconsistencies need care.** Some older portfolio pages display a platform publication timestamp of 18 December 2025 while the same card also shows age labels such as "13 years ago" or "6 months ago". Use the platform's explicit created/published fields when visible, retain the raw discrepancy, and do not infer a reliable last-activity date from search snippets alone.
5. **Cross-platform presence is wider than the verified content inventory.** LinkedIn, YouTube, VK Video and RUTUBE have previously recorded public presences. Instagram, Facebook and Dzen URLs were supplied by the user, but their pages were not independently inspected in this pass. Ownership/admin access remains unverified across these surfaces.

## Content architecture: preliminary recommendations

These are editorial recommendations, not instructions to publish or edit existing profiles.

- Replace generic titles such as "Clip 1" with accurate, human-readable titles that state the service, use case and language where known.
- Group portfolio assets into stable service themes: advertising audio, voice-over for video, dubbing/localization, IVR/telephone greetings, voice-talent demos, audio branding/jingles, and studio/service explainers. Only use a theme when the actual asset supports it.
- For each portfolio item, record client/project, deliverable, language, voice talent, production role, duration, publication rights, source asset, and a short factual case description. Omit fields that cannot be verified.
- Link a case to its original canonical page or portfolio entry; identify reposts/cross-posts rather than counting them as separate original works.
- Keep current tariffs and delivery terms in a separately approved source of truth. Historical platform descriptions must not override current prices.
- Use platform-native analytics only when an authorized account owner exports or shares them. Public view counts are not substitutes for reach, watch time, retention, clicks, leads or conversions.
- Do not assume that a video with a company-branded creator label is controlled by the company. Verify account access and rights before changing, promoting or reusing assets.
