# Attention Surface Register — 2026-11-17

This register converts the attention-surface candidates researched in the ecosystem reconnaissance into a filterable operational inventory.

## Scope and guardrails

- Brand: **Аудио-Реклама•ru**; canonical public website: https://www.audio-reclama.ru.
- This is a research register, not evidence of an existing listing, account, profile, submission, publication, or commercial relationship.
- No registration, submission, journalist response, booking, payment, paid upgrade, or external publication was performed.
- A free listing claim is recorded only where the reviewed official source explicitly supports it. "Not confirmed" must not be rewritten as free.
- Eligibility is conditional on the real service offering and verified business facts. Do not invent a physical studio, address, equipment, capacity, hourly rate, staff credentials, or an individual expert identity.
- An empty indexed search is not proof that a listing is absent. Current listing state remains `NOT_CHECKED` unless directly verified in the platform's own directory.
- Review platform terms and content/IP rights before submitting any original material.

## Additional candidates added 2026-11-17

Six additional surfaces were added after a second discovery pass:

- **Podle** — podcast/video services directory; company listing is stated free. Conditional on a genuine match to its podcast/video service taxonomy. Review its terms and check whether the company already appears.
- **Enterprise League** — B2B directory with an Audio Production category and country pages. Standard profile is stated free; verification may be free but paid subscription is required for the verified badge to display in directory/search results. Treat the free profile and paid visibility badge separately.
- **Podwires** — international podcast producer marketplace; free profiles are for individual producers/freelancers, not a confirmed company listing route. Do not create a company-as-person profile.
- **VoiceLab360** — studio partner listing stated free, but only for qualifying professional studios, home studios, remote-session studios or production facilities with voice capability. A 2.5% platform booking fee applies to completed bookings processed there; platform terms require those bookings to remain on-platform.
- **Podcasting Source** — invites studios, software, agencies and services to list, but free pricing and submission terms have not been confirmed. Keep unpriced until checked.
- **GetListedGlobal** — generic international business directory with a free standard listing and account requirement. Low priority until country support, category quality, data terms and buyer relevance are checked.

A separate exact-brand/domain discovery pass surfaced three existing public company/agency pages. These are **observed indexed presences**, not evidence that the business owns or can edit the profiles:

- **Cinematch** — public company profile at `https://www.cinematch.pro/companies/audio%2Dreclama-ru`; page labels the profile as confirmed and displays company, location, service and contact details. Verify what the platform's confirmation means and who controls the page.
- **LinkedIn** — public company page at `https://www.linkedin.com/company/audio%2Dreclama-ru`; it contains organization description and company metadata. Treat employee-count, founding-year and other profile fields as page claims until verified. Check administrator access before edits.
- **Ruward** — public agency catalog profile at `https://ruward.ru/catalog/audio%2Dreclama-ru/`; it contains company description, categories, projects and contact details. Some historical claims (including voice-talent counts and founding/operating dates) need primary-source review before reuse.

The initial indexed search did not surface exact-match results for VORG or The VoiceOver Community. This is **not proof of absence**. Their `listing_status` remains `NOT_CHECKED` until the native directory search is inspected manually. More generally, search-engine indexing and platform-native listing state are separate evidence fields.

## CSV fields

- `platform`, `url`: platform identity and reviewed entry point.
- `surface_type`, `discovery_mechanism`: what kind of attention surface it is and how people discover entries.
- `geographic_scope`: verified or qualified geographic reach; unknown coverage remains unknown.
- `free_listing_claim`, `paid_boundary`: explicit free claims and any separate paid/commission boundary.
- `eligibility`, `fit_for_audio_reclama_ru`: requirements and conditional fit assessment.
- `evidence_strength`, `source_urls`: official evidence used for the assessment.
- `rights_and_data_caveats`: material terms, rights, or factual constraints.
- `listing_status`: whether an existing public listing was checked; currently not checked.
- `verification_date`: date of this research snapshot, not proof of a live account.
- `next_action`, `action_status`: safe next research step and confirmation that no external action was taken.

## Status vocabulary

- `NOT_CHECKED`: existing public listing has not been directly checked in the platform's own directory.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE_AS_LISTING`: the surface is not a directory listing destination.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE_TO_COMPANY_PROFILE`: the platform's profile type does not fit a company profile.
- `RESEARCH_ONLY`: no account, submission, response, booking, or publication has been made.

## Next research gate

Before preparing any profile copy, verify (1) exact category fit against the actual offer, (2) current directory presence using the platform's own search, (3) country/region eligibility, (4) free-tier limits and paid boundaries, and (5) terms governing submitted text, images, and intellectual property. Prepare factual draft copy only after these checks; do not submit without explicit authorization.
