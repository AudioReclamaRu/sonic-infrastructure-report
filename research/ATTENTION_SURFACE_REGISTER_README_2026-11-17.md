# Attention Surface Register — 2026-11-17

This register converts the attention-surface candidates researched in the ecosystem reconnaissance into a filterable operational inventory. It now separates potential listing opportunities from existing public brand presences and third-party data mentions.

## Scope and guardrails

- Brand: **Аудио-Реклама•ru**; canonical public website: https://www.audio-reclama.ru.
- This is a research register, not evidence of an existing listing, account, profile, submission, publication, or commercial relationship.
- No registration, submission, journalist response, booking, payment, paid upgrade, external publication, profile claim or edit was performed.
- A free listing claim is recorded only where the reviewed official source explicitly supports it. "Not confirmed" must not be rewritten as free.
- Eligibility is conditional on the real service offering and verified business facts. Do not invent a physical studio, address, equipment, capacity, hourly rate, staff credentials, or an individual expert identity.
- An empty indexed search is not proof that a listing is absent. Current listing state remains `NOT_CHECKED` unless directly verified in the platform's own directory.
- Review platform terms and content/IP rights before submitting any original material.
- Third-party data aggregators and search snippets are not canonical company records. Their estimates must not be promoted into factual company claims.

## CSV fields

- `platform`, `url`: platform identity and reviewed entry point.
- `surface_type`, `discovery_mechanism`: what kind of attention surface it is and how people discover entries.
- `geographic_scope`: verified or qualified geographic reach; unknown coverage remains unknown.
- `free_listing_claim`, `paid_boundary`: explicit free claims and any separate paid/commission boundary.
- `eligibility`, `fit_for_audio_reclama_ru`: requirements and conditional fit assessment.
- `evidence_strength`, `source_urls`: official or direct public evidence used for the assessment.
- `rights_and_data_caveats`: material terms, rights, or factual constraints.
- `listing_status`: state of the public presence as observed; this does not prove ownership or admin control.
- `verification_date`: date of this research snapshot, not proof of a live account.
- `next_action`, `action_status`: safe next research step and confirmation that no external action was taken.

## Status vocabulary

- `NOT_CHECKED`: existing public listing has not been directly checked in the platform's own directory.
- `FOUND_PUBLIC_LISTING`: a public listing/profile/media presence was directly observed; ownership and edit rights may remain unknown.
- `MENTION_ONLY`: a third-party mention or data-aggregator record exists, but it is not treated as a canonical or owner-managed profile.
- `STALE_OR_UNVERIFIED`: observed content appears stale, conflicting or unverified; do not reuse its claims without primary-source checks.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE_AS_LISTING`: the surface is not a directory listing destination.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE_TO_COMPANY_PROFILE`: the platform's profile type does not fit a company profile.

## Next research gate

Before preparing any profile copy, verify (1) exact category fit against the actual offer, (2) current directory presence using the platform's own search, (3) country/region eligibility, (4) free-tier limits and paid boundaries, and (5) terms governing submitted text, images, and intellectual property.

For existing presences, first verify (1) identity and legal-entity match, (2) factual freshness of address, contacts, services and portfolio, (3) profile owner/admin access, (4) source and rights of media/assets, and (5) whether public metrics indicate meaningful audience. Record unresolved conflicts; do not make edits or claims without explicit authorization.
