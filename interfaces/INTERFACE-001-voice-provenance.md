## Interface

The boundary of origin: where a voice is proven to come from a specific human
at a specific time — or is not.

## Pipeline

```
Input (recording session)
  ↓
Editorial Check
  ↓
Responsible Release
  ↓
Output (voice asset with provenance record)
```

## What it prevents

- A synthetic or repurposed voice being presented as a verified human recording
- An undated, unsourced quality claim in a commercial offer
